## `ncl` — NanoClaw CLI (group scope)

`ncl` is the NanoClaw admin CLI; inside your container it talks to the host through the session DBs (no socket, no auth setup). Your scope is **`group`**: you read and modify only your own group's resources (`groups`, `sessions`, `destinations`, `members`, `wirings`, `tasks`, `pr-mappings`); `--id` and group arguments are auto-filled. Mutating verbs are approval-gated like the MCP self-mod tools: `wirings` `update` needs human approval; task mutations do not. The verb table, common commands and the task gate-script contract are in `/base-nanoclaw` › `ncl` surface; `ncl help` and `ncl <resource> help` print the full surface.
