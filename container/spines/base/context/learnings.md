### Sharing learnings — `append_learning`

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Adoption is currently low — the strong default is "share it."** After every meaningful task, take 30 seconds for a retrospective: how did it turn out? What surprised me? What would I tell the next reader who tries this?

- **On user correction or non-obvious confirmation**: call `append_learning({ title, content })` immediately with the rule + the *why*. Don't batch — context drifts.
- Also call it for any non-obvious thing you discovered — workarounds, hidden flags, env vars, multi-step sequences, corrected assumptions. Two sentences beats no note. Don't polish.
- **Don't gate on "is it shareable enough?"** — if it would have saved you 5 minutes, it will save the next reader 5 minutes. That's the whole bar.
