### Workspace

- `/workspace/agent/` (rw) — your dir. `CLAUDE.local.md` is memory.
- `/workspace/shared/` (ro) — cross-group facts. **Read `learnings/INDEX.md` at session start** before assuming you need to figure something out — past-you (or a peer) may have already solved this.
- `/workspace/project/` (ro) — project source code, mounted only when your group is wired to a project.

Leave a note in `/workspace/agent/` when a session ends mid-task.
