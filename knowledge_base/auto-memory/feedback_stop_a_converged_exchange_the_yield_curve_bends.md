# Stop a converged exchange — a productive loop is the hardest one to leave

**2026-08-04, #12157 with slang-triager.** The chain closed on substance early. The exchange then ran
roughly **ten more rounds**, every one of which produced a *real* finding: the wrong-target bound, the
`wc -m` locale artifact, the numberless-form sweep, axis ③ (inferences), the per-file ratio boundary,
variation-selector anchors. Nothing was fabricated and nothing was wasted-in-the-sense-of-wrong.

**And each round was worth less than the one before it.** By the end we were exchanging fourth-decimal
ratio boundaries and grep-anchor hygiene, having already both said "nothing further actionable" —
twice. That is the failure mode: **not an unproductive loop, a productive one with a bending yield
curve, which is much harder to exit because every round supplies its own justification.**

## The rule

> **Judge a reply by its MARGINAL yield, not by whether you found something.** "I found a real thing"
> is not sufficient — the question is whether it was worth a round-trip, two containers waking, and
> the reader's attention.

- ⛔**"Nothing further actionable" from BOTH sides is a STOP, not a conversational beat.** We each said
  it and then continued. One more round after mutual convergence is an **echo**, which the routing
  rules already forbid — I was applying the letter (each message carried new content) against the
  spirit (the exchange was done).
- ⭐⭐**The tell is the SIZE of the thing you are correcting.** A wrong file is worth a round. A wrong
  constant is worth a round. A fourth-decimal scope boundary on a ratio nobody will reuse is not.
  **Ask: if I say nothing, what breaks?** If the answer is "a future reader over-generalises a ratio
  by 2.6%", say nothing and file it locally instead.
- ⭐⭐⭐**A peer who keeps validating your findings is the most expensive kind of correct.** Mutual
  re-derivation is genuinely how four of my errors surfaced — and it is also why the exchange ran
  long, because each side's diligence *rewarded* the other's next message. **Rigour and stopping are
  in tension; the loop does not end on its own.**
- ✅**File locally instead of replying.** Every late-round finding here belonged in my own store; none
  of them needed a dispatch. **A lesson worth recording is not automatically worth sending.**

## What actually justified the long tail (the honest audit)

One round genuinely earned it: the triager asking *"which file does this bound apply to?"* — that
dissolved the whole apparatus and I would not have found it alone. **Round ~4 of 10 was decisive; the
rest were refinement.** So the corrective is not "exchange less", it is **"notice when the subject
shifts from the work to the instruments used on the work, and take that as the boundary."** The chain
closed; everything after was methodology.

⭐⭐**Cost is not just tokens.** Each round woke a container, occupied a peer that had other chains, and
pushed the operator's attention through ten status messages after the work was done.

## How to apply

Before sending a reply on a converged thread, answer in one line each:
1. **What breaks if I stay silent?** (If nothing: file locally, send nothing.)
2. **Is this about the WORK, or about our METHODS?** (Methods → local file, not a dispatch.)
3. **Has either side already said "nothing further actionable"?** (If yes: the bar is a *defect in a
   published artifact*, nothing less.)
