# slang #11591/#11590 — prior 41303-to-Slice-3 ruling (1781318517600) re-verified VALID at HEAD a84f48e62 post-#11594; carrying 41303 in Slice 2 is a confirmed band-aid

When a held multi-slice chain is unblocked and resumed in a later session, RE-RUN the Recall step before re-deciding — a prior ruling on the exact question can be contradicted by a "fresh" analysis that lacks it. And re-verify that prior ruling at current HEAD rather than assuming it's stale.

**Concrete case (2026-06-18):** On unblocking #11591 (Slice 2 of #11545), I "discovered a soundness gap" (#11594 shipped only the 41302/constexpr half of the contract, no 41303) and recommended Option 1 = carry 41303 inside the Slice-2 PR #11595. That CONTRADICTED my own prior IR-dump-proven ruling 1781318517600 (2026-06-13, "Option A"), which I missed by skipping re-Recall. slang-reviewer caught it.

**Re-verification at current HEAD a84f48e62 (post-#11594) — Option A STILL HOLDS, #11594 did NOT moot it:**
- Single-arg accessors still forward `__naturalStrideOf<T>` as the alignment operand (hlsl.meta.slang:489-491; Load2/3/4 :285-291/:362-368/:439-445; RW set :6247/6324/6401/6470; Store :7159). #11594 added `constexpr uint alignment` to the 2-arg form only.
- `__naturalAlignmentOf` / `getNaturalAlignment` does NOT exist in master — it is Slice 3 (#11592)'s producer fix.
- `simplifyIR` folds the alignment operand to a literal before the legalize pass, so at the legalize entry a forwarded natural STRIDE is indistinguishable from an explicit user promise — there is NO IR marker to scope a 41303 check to explicit-promises-only.
- ⇒ An in-Slice-2 41303 false-rejects valid `Load3Aligned(16)` / `LoadAligned<float3>(16)` (uint3 stride = 12; 16 % 12 ≠ 0, though 16 is a fine 4-aligned location). PR #11595 masked this by re-expressing the valid calls (`Load3Aligned(16)`→`(16,4)`, `LoadAligned<float4>(8)`→`(8,4)`) — the exact "do not mask" band-aid Option A rejected.

**How to apply:** for ByteAddressBuffer alignment, 41303 ("constant location multiple of alignment") cannot live in any slice that runs at/after the post-simplifyIR legalize entry UNTIL the single-arg producer forwards true natural ALIGNMENT (Slice 3 #11592, `__naturalStrideOf`→`__naturalAlignmentOf`). So 41303 couples with Slice 3, and Slice 2's consistency change DEPENDS ON Slice 3 (Slice 2 must not merge before Slice 3). Carrying 41303 earlier either false-rejects 3-component single-arg loads or requires masking. The 41302/constexpr half IS slice-safe (a folded literal passes; only a dynamic explicit alignment trips it). General lesson: a "soundness gap" you spot on resume may already have a prior, better-evidenced ruling that your fix is re-deriving as a band-aid.
