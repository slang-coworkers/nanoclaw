## TL;DR

- A claim about live state, made from a read taken earlier and phrased in the present tense, is the recurring bug. Before quoting any state, ask: *what would change this, and has it had time to happen?*
- Re-read terminal state (PR merged/closed, head SHA) in the *same step* that writes a join line — a long-running review outlives the state it describes.
- A composite scraped artifact has a freshness *per region*, not per document; sum the parts and check they equal the whole (a page can render live sidebar metadata over a cached analysis body).
- A mutating bot artifact (CodeRabbit's single edited summary comment) makes `created_at` meaningless for currency and "I already read that" unsafe — judge currency by the Commits range it names, not the timestamp; ask of any bot artifact whether it appends or mutates.
- Temporal-ordering claims about your own files are measurable facts — `stat`/`date -u -r` them, never narrate them. "Recorded before the result was known" and "I disclose my decision is contaminated" are both claims about mtimes, and both flatter you when guessed.
- An always-loaded index is not a source for any figure; give every number in a long-lived document its measurement date, or it becomes a false claim by the passage of time alone.
- A late scheduled wake is indistinguishable from a quiet one: the damage of a late wake equals a missed one but reports as success. Print `now − process_after` at every wake.
- Alarms on ratios/deltas fail vacuously where absolutes do not: a queue-depth alarm is anti-correlated with a total capacity outage. Alarm on oldest-queued age and `total == 0`.
- A "someone else owns this" suppression needs a liveness predicate on the owner; an open PR read as "a human has the ball" makes a dead chain invisible by construction. A reclaimable-state window is a *band*, not a deadline — place staleness checks past the upper bound.
- A resolution error in your own tool log is a receipt you will read past — "salvaged"/"partial" phrasing converts a hard failure into an ignorable line. Grep your own run log before nudging on "nothing found."
- A burst of bounced handoffs is one incident with N victims: re-drive all, diagnose once; a long-open bot draft PR can be silently overtaken — diff before defending it.

## Live state outlives the read that sampled it

The general failure is a snapshot presented as current. [A PR can reach its terminal state while your decision is still running](../learnings/1786388078044-a-pr-can-reach-its-terminal-state-while-your-decis.md): a decision artifact instructed "record the human verdict on merge/close" for a PR that had *already merged* two hours earlier. Re-read terminal state (`gh pr view --json state,mergedAt,mergeCommit,headRefOid`) in the same step that writes the join line, check whether the merged head equals the SHA you decided, and prefer "join is available now" over a pending instruction nobody will execute. The trigger generalizes in [Before quoting any state, ask what would MUTATE it](../learnings/1786391523179-approver-challenger-miss-before-quoting-any-state-.md): a field named `*_at`, `*_attempts`, `*_count`, `retries`, `pending`, `expires` is a declaration the record mutates — treat reading one as a subscription, not a measurement. That atom also separates *stale snapshot* (correct when read, changed after) from *wrong artifact* (static documentation — hook stderr, comments — quoted as dynamic runtime behavior; re-reading the same artifact never catches it). Quote the artifact's own recorded timestamp field, never the ambient session clock, which "drifts monotonically in the unflattering direction."

## Freshness is per-region, and mutating artifacts defeat timestamps

A scraped page is not one revision. [Date a scraped page by summing its parts, not by reading its header](../learnings/1786390321177-approver-challenger-miss-date-a-scraped-page-by-su.md): a Devin capture showed a correct R2 totals line (`+274 −9`) over an R1 analysis body — live sidebar metadata rendered atop a cached analysis. Sum the section figures and check they equal the whole; a composite artifact has a freshness per region, and if nothing constrains the region you care about, record it *unattested* and exclude it in both directions. The prior framing ("the first capture after a push is the old rev") was insufficient — it can be *both at once*, and the fresh half is the half you check first. Relatedly, [CodeRabbit EDITS ITS SUMMARY COMMENT IN PLACE](../learnings/1786398325966-approver-infra-abstain-coderabbit-edits-its-summar.md): one summary comment is rewritten on every revision, so `created_at` says nothing about which revision the body describes, and the *incremental* range means "no actionable comments" covers only the latest delta. Judge currency by the "Commits" range the body names; check `created_at != updated_at`; and for any bot artifact ask whether the tool appends or mutates — a mutating one makes a remembered reading unsafe across revisions.

## Temporal ordering is a measurable fact, not a state of mind

Two atoms cover claims about your own artifacts' timestamps. [Never assert a temporal ordering between your own artifacts without reading their mtimes](../learnings/1786403847222-approver-critique-mustfix-never-assert-a-temporal-.md): "recorded before the result was known" was false — a `stat` showed the disposition file was written 22 s *after* the background run's result-bearing artifacts. A process guarantee is a claim about artifacts, not about your state of mind; to earn a pre-commitment, write the disposition *before dispatching* the run and cite both mtimes. [Disclosing that your decision is outcome-contaminated is itself a claim about your own artifacts](../learnings/1786412553806-approver-false-safe-disclosing-that-your-decision-.md) is the sharper version: a disclosure that "my artifacts postdate the merge by minutes" understated the lateness by ~16 minutes and missed that the *review input itself* postdated the merge (the capture literally rendered `Merged`). Having *noticed* contamination felt like having *handled* it — the diligence-slot failure in pure form. Distinguish "write-up postdates the outcome" (recoverable) from "the evidence itself postdates it" (a dirty stage); `date -u -r` every "X happened at T" about your own files; grep captured evidence for terminal-state words; and record the state with a named reason code (`STALE_STAGE`) rather than a prose caveat that gets scored like a normal row.

## Figures in long-lived documents decay silently

[An always-loaded index is not a source for any figure](../learnings/1786396698211-an-always-loaded-index-is-not-a-source-for-any-fig.md): compaction preserved a rule ("7 instances") and dropped the seven specific instances behind it, and a root index stating "667 entries" was a five-day-old measurement reading as current (actual 1,194). Give every figure in a persistent artifact its measurement date, or delete it — an unanchored number converts into a false claim by the passage of time alone. Companion rules: re-derive a duration, never carry it (publish "served no work for 3.9h and counting" *with the anchor timestamp*); and "safe direction" is a property of one instance, not of the mistake (an 89-minute error that happened to overstate an outage would understate a mirror-case outage identically).

## Monitoring: silence looks like calm

Scheduled instruments fail by reading absence as health. [A late scheduled wake is indistinguishable from a quiet one](../learnings/1786395212955-a-late-scheduled-wake-is-indistinguishable-from-a-.md): a heartbeat delivered 3h22m late left Discord unscanned while every in-agent freshness signal looked fine — a stale-frame check cannot detect a stale *wake*. The one cheap discriminator is `now − process_after`; establish *when you were supposed to run* before scanning from the watermark, and widen the scan window to cover the lag. (Bonus in that atom: a `?status=completed&per_page=30` page with zero runs of the target workflow was the *wrong corpus*, not an absence.) [A queue-depth alarm is anti-correlated with a total capacity outage](../learnings/1786395574061-a-queue-depth-alarm-is-anti-correlated-with-a-tota.md): during a 3.5h runner outage the `jobs_queued > 30` alarm read 20 because depth climbs only while servers are busy and flatlines when they vanish — alarm on oldest-queued age (monotone in badness) and `total == 0`. `in_progress == 0` while `queued > 0` is the outage signature and needs no privileged endpoint, but it does not establish *why* (autoscaler vs offline) — report the effect with the mechanism explicitly unattributed.

Supervision suppression can make a dead chain permanently invisible. [A reclaimable-state window is a BAND, not a deadline](../learnings/1786409172570-a-reclaimable-state-window-is-a-band-not-a-deadlin.md): a CI yield is reclaimable between a 12h eligibility threshold and a 16h retention horizon, so a "terminal after ~12h" test false-positives exactly when the mechanism is about to work — enumerate both bounds and place the check past the upper one. Its second defect: an open PR read as "a human has the ball" short-circuited before any staleness logic ran, so 30 dead review chains could never be nudged; a "someone else owns this" suppression needs a liveness predicate on the owner (here `reviewDecision == REVIEW_REQUIRED` ∧ own-bot reviews `== 0` ∧ PR open). And [A resolution error in your own tool log is a receipt you will read past](../learnings/1786409471508-a-resolution-error-in-your-own-tool-log-is-a-recei.md): three nudges on false "no GitHub artifact" premises were refuted by GraphQL "Could not resolve to a Repository" lines in the instrument's own stderr 20 minutes earlier — the word "salvaged" converted a hard failure into an ignorable soft line. "Not found" and "I asked the wrong question" render identically; grep your own run log before nudging, count what your parser dropped, and assert the producer→consumer seam (a field the consumer reads and the producer never writes needs a non-empty boundary assertion).

## One incident, N victims; and a PR that went stale under you

[A burst of bounced handoffs is one incident with N victims](../learnings/1786388767272-a-burst-of-bounced-handoffs-is-one-incident-with-n.md): four `bounced-unknown` a2a redrives in one minute were one provider-side outage — re-drive all, diagnose once (per-chain diagnosis produces four unrelated wrong stories). Re-driving a message you cannot read means *asking*, not fabricating the lost instruction; look up each victim's live state first (one chain had zero comments for seven weeks — no public artifact at all). Finally, [A long-open bot draft PR can be silently overtaken by a human PR](../learnings/1786435268372-a-long-open-bot-draft-pr-can-be-silently-overtaken.md): a 4-week-old bot draft's `mergeable: CONFLICTING / mergeStateStatus: DIRTY` on a previously-green PR is the tell that someone edited the same lines — on a duplicate fix the conflict is *exactly* your changed lines. Diff against `main` and cite file:line before defending; closing the duplicate is not the whole job (correct the originating issue's stale "fix incoming in #N" pointer, and state the residual scope — the superseding PR landed the code but not the test or docs).
