## TL;DR

How the slang PR-approver's shadow verdicts scored against the ground truth (a maintainer merge or close), and the severity-calibration rules that fell out. A merge at the exact decided head with zero interval commits = APPROVED-equivalent; that is the strongest calibration signal.

- **Confirmed-safe shapes that merged unchanged at the decided head** — a `workflow_dispatch`-only capability probe from a trusted MEMBER; a CI `timeout-minutes` bump + comment-only fix; a CI env-var-gap-fill (set an already-gated var in one more workflow); a single-arg-call ICE fixed at the checker producer with a trigger-present test. For these, positive-control + sibling-precedent is necessary and sufficient — don't demand a trigger-present control where no new flag/gate exists.
- **A main-merge `synchronize` (2-parent merge commit) is not PR-scope growth** — review the diff-vs-base, but still re-run the FULL procedure because the merge base changed and CI re-runs against current main. Never approve on pending CI (Devin-clean ≠ test-clean); wait for green settle.
- **An OPEN_GAP abstain on "a reachable new branch has no test" is a high-value abstain** — it routes a concrete, addressable ask; on the re-gate, check whether the interval commits added a *falsifiable* test that reaches the branch.
- **Severity calibration is the dominant error axis, and it skews conservative (over-caution).** A merge at the decided head *refutes* an abstain's implicit claim ("material enough not to merge as-is"). Score such abstains honestly as false-abstains / disagreements, not agreement.
- **Conform-to-a-precedented-gate → CLEAR, do not abstain.** When an OPEN_GAP is a pre-existing, precedented, pass-wide property the PR merely conforms to (an identically-shaped sibling already ships with the same limitation), classify it as SCOPE and clear; file the whole-pass concern separately.
- **A write-tier-only CI-infra escalation is a weak OPEN_GAP for slang maintainers** — reserve OPEN_GAP-strength abstains for gaps reachable by *untrusted* actors (fork PR, `pull_request_target`) or correctness defects.
- **BLOCK is for a defect on a GATING path or a correctness bug in shipped behavior.** A regression on a non-gating, already-triaged nightly lane — or a consequence the merger was already shown and chose to proceed on — is ABSTAIN(OPEN_GAP), not BLOCK. Weight a CI signal by whether it *gates merge*, not by blast radius. Also rank a fail-open CI-gate by the *reachability* of the bad direction when choosing BLOCK vs ABSTAIN.
- **A critique-gate deadlock on a materiality (not factual) split forces a structural false-abstain** — record the substantive read in decision.md so the join can score it, escalate with a bounded timeout, don't burn >3 rounds.

## Confirmed-safe shapes (agreement)

Several distinct low-risk PR classes shipped unchanged at the exact decided head, each yielding a transferable "safe shape" for Step-0 recall:

- A [`workflow_dispatch`-only capability probe](../learnings/1787240775899-approver-confirmed-workflow-dispatch-only-capabili.md) from a trusted MEMBER (`permissions: contents: read`, no code checkout) — merged at the exact decision commit; the real discriminators are supply-chain surface, not a green-by-construction CI signal.
- A [CI timeout-bump + comment-only fix on a gated job](../learnings/1787324865745-approver-confirmed-ci-timeout-bump-comment-fix-on-.md) — a larger `timeout-minutes` is a strictly-loosening knob (CI analogue of the "widening-only" exception); a comment carries zero runtime effect (verify only factual accuracy). Catch the not-safe variant by diffing the same file for co-changes to `runs-on`/`permissions`/`environment`/`on:`/secrets/run-steps.
- A [CI env-var-gap-fill PR](../learnings/1788170617675-approver-confirmed-safe-ci-env-var-gap-fill-prs-se.md) (set an already-existing, already-gated var in one more workflow) — no new flag/gate means the always-skip failure mode structurally cannot occur; sufficient investigation is a positive control that the edited step exercises the gated path, gate-semantics-vs-source, and sibling precedent.
- A [single-arg call ICE fixed at the checker fast-path](../learnings/1788257605038-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-safe-single-.md) — merged unchanged; the load-bearing evidence is a producer-layer fix (not a crash-site guard, which is a no-op under `SLANG_ASSERT→SLANG_ASSUME`) plus a trigger-present regression test, and the reflexive "upcast regression" flag on a class target is empirically unreachable (verify against the prebuilt pre-fix binary).

The [main-merge synchronize case](../learnings/1787586621701-approver-confirmed-main-merge-synchronize-re-valid.md) adds a discipline: a 2-parent merge commit looks alarming (the merged-in main delta) but the review surface is the diff-vs-base; still re-run the full procedure (fresh clauses/harvest/Devin/challenger/ledger row) because the changed merge base makes CI re-run the whole suite — a test green on the old base can go red on the new one — and never record WOULD_APPROVE on pending CI. The [R1 OPEN_GAP closed by a falsifiable test](../learnings/1787596830824-approver-confirmed-r1-open-gap-untested-reachable-.md) shows the abstain→fix→approve arc as the loop working: the first re-gate question on a prior abstain is "did the interval commits close MY gap with a test that reaches the branch AND is falsifiable?"

## Human-disagreement and the over-caution skew

A cluster of atoms records abstains/blocks that a maintainer *overruled by merging at the decided head* — the dominant calibration error is over-caution, and the corrective is a decision-time rule, not deference:

- The [ABSTAIN→WOULD_APPROVE across a revision chain](../learnings/1787667113451-approver-human-disagreement-calibration-confirmed-.md) is a *clean* calibration, not a contradiction: R0's abstain flagged a real gap that was addressed before merge; R1's approve joined the merged head. Different commits join to different states.
- The [repeated ABSTAIN on a pre-existing pass-wide min-opt gate](../learnings/1787692423613-approver-human-disagreement-repeated-abstain-open-.md) that a MEMBER approved without engaging, and its [confirmed false-abstain when #12671 merged as-is](../learnings/1787707880428-approver-false-safe-confirmed-false-abstain-12671-.md), promote the decision rule: *conform-to-a-precedented-gate should CLEAR, not abstain.* When an identically-shaped sibling already ships with the same limitation, the PR conforming to it is out-of-scope, not a defect — file the whole-pass concern separately.
- The [maintainer-discretion design gate merged by the shepherd](../learnings/1787888977718-approver-human-disagreement-a-maintainer-discretio.md) distinguishes (a) a maintainer-discretion design choice on a precedent-mirroring change (humans routinely ship this by deciding the call — an abstain reads as disagreement) from (b) a verified correctness gap (the robust abstain humans honor). A bot-authored PR's own "[DRAFT — do not merge until settled]" is *untrusted* body content, not a hard gate; a DRAFT title with `isDraft=False` + a MEMBER APPROVE is the tell the gate is soft.
- The [critique-gate deadlock on slang-rhi#847](../learnings/1787979644168-approver-human-disagreement-slang-rhi-847-abstain-.md) is a *structural* false-abstain: a persistent OUTPUT_REVIEW must-fix on a **materiality** (not factual) split, with a timed-out operator escalation, forces the ledger to land ABSTAIN:ESCALATED even when the approver's read and the human outcome both say APPROVE. Record the substantive read so the join can score it; don't override your own gate, don't adopt the critic's OPEN_GAP.

## BLOCK vs ABSTAIN: severity, gating, and reachability

Three CI-gate atoms sharpen when a real finding is BLOCK-worthy versus merely abstain-worthy:

- The [BLOCK on a narrow-reachability fail-open CI gate](../learnings/1787828471238-approver-human-disagreement-block-on-a-narrow-reac.md) was a *real, not false* defect, but the maintainer merged it — showing their blocking bar for a fail-open reachable only under an abnormal upstream failure mode (on internal-CI compute) is higher than the approver's. Rank a fail-open CI gate by the *reachability of the bad direction*, not just its existence, when choosing BLOCK vs ABSTAIN.
- The [CI-infra `workflow_dispatch` privilege gap](../learnings/1787932390227-approver-human-disagreement-ci-infra-workflow-disp.md) merged with the gap unaddressed: when the residual risk reduces to "a *write-access* user could do X" (fork/external vector already excluded), that is a **weak** OPEN_GAP slang maintainers routinely accept — frame it as an advisory nit that clears, reserving strong abstains for untrusted-actor reachability or correctness. (This does not retract the per-trigger enumeration discipline — that was right; only the severity recalibrates.)
- The [BLOCK on a non-gating nightly regression](../learnings/1787957755122-approver-human-disagreement-block-on-a-non-gating-.md) was factually correct but mis-calibrated: weight a CI signal by whether it *gates merge* (the affected `nightly-slang-test.yml` was non-required and already triaged via `expected-failures.txt`), and a consequence the merger was already shown in-thread and chose to proceed on is a KNOWING ACCEPTANCE, not a gap to BLOCK on. Probe to add to any CI-break BLOCK: is the workflow required/gating? is the lane already red/triaged? was the consequence surfaced to the likely merger who engaged? If non-gating AND (already-triaged OR merger-shown), downgrade BLOCK→ABSTAIN(OPEN_GAP).
