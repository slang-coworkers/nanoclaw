## Incremental catches & calibration (2026-07-18/19 approver session)

**A `synchronize` is not automatically a fix — classify the delta before re-litigating.** Three sharpenings of the per-revision discipline: (1) a `synchronize` whose new head is a pure `Merge branch 'master'` changes nothing in the PR's own content — verify the flagged files' git **blob-sha** across the two heads (`gh api contents/<path>?ref=<sha> --jq .sha`) plus `additions/deletions/changedFiles`; identical blob ⇒ any prior finding persists verbatim, re-BLOCK/re-hold without re-deriving ([[approver/calibration] A "synchronize" that is a pure master-merge fixes nothing — verify blob-sha equality before assuming a re-push addressed prior findings](../learnings/1784366423621-approver-calibration-a-synchronize-that-is-a-pure-.md)). (2) For a master-merge on a PR with **hot files**, whole-file blob SHAs will differ because master independently edited those files — the authoritative check is the PR **net-diff hunks** (`gh pr diff`), not the blob; and the operative human signal is the APPROVED review's `commit.oid`, read it (don't infer the association from `submittedAt` vs `committedDate` ordering) ([[approver/challenger] master-merge synchronize: verify fix net-diff byte-identical … check the APPROVED review's commit.oid — slang#12133 R4](../learnings/1784383023678-approver-challenger-master-merge-synchronize-verif.md)). (3) A push that "addresses review" may address a **different axis** than your open gap — re-run the challenger against the SPECIFIC gap that drove the prior withhold (diff the exact code region, check a test now covers YOUR trigger shape, check the maintainer decision you were waiting on actually landed); "new test added" / "comment resolved" is about whatever the reviewer asked, which may be orthogonal ([[approver/calibration] A synchronize addressing a different review axis does not close your open gap](../learnings/1784421277721-approver-calibration-a-synchronize-addressing-a-di.md)). (4) A "Fix …" commit may patch only ONE of N near-duplicate call sites — on slangpy#1075 R7 the exact off-by-one fix (`(i+1) % BATCH_SIZE` + `device->wait()`) landed on `create_textures` while the structurally-identical sibling `create_texture_array` kept BOTH the old form AND no `device->wait()`; the inconsistency is itself a tell that the author knew the fix and didn't propagate it. Never infer "gap fixed" from a commit message or one reviewer no longer flagging it — grep the whole file for the pattern and read EVERY loop body at head, blob-SHA-comparing the reviewed file (`gh api contents/<path>?ref=<sha> --jq .sha`) for material-vs-no-op. Calibration nuance: when a second reviewer (CodeRabbit) later corroborates Devin AND supplies the missing mechanism (per-item `generate_mips()` inside the unwaited batch loop), that falsifies a "maybe it doesn't hit the mechanism" hedge and confirms the gap is real and reachable — but a load/driver-dependent resource-exhaustion RACE that can't be reproduced still stays OPEN_GAP (a hard-fail needs a VERIFIED, ideally reproduced, defect), don't round a firmed-up gap up OR down ([[approver/challenger] Partial-fix revision: one sibling patched, the other left inconsistent — verify EACH call site, don't assume a fix propagated](../learnings/1785490973077-approver-challenger-partial-fix-revision-one-sibli.md)). The mirror on the CI side: a forwarded CI *failure* is stamped with the head it ran against — resolve its `head_sha` (`gh api check-suites/<id> --jq .head_sha`) vs your pinned settled head before treating it as live; on a fast-moving PR the author may have pushed past the failure ([[approver/calibration] forwarded-CI-failure-suite-may-be-at-superseded-head](../learnings/1784416965336-approver-calibration-forwarded-ci-failure-suite-ma.md)). And "head advanced past your verdict" is frequently just **master-catchup** (`behind_by:0`, commits are other already-merged PRs) — diff the delta and confirm the PR's OWN load-bearing files are byte-identical before spending a full re-harvest+re-Devin+CI cycle; a mid-chain design *revert* resets the verdict (evaluate the reverted-to design on its own merits) ([[approver/human-agreement] Full-chain vindication: BLOCK→fix→APPROVE held through merge; and "head advanced" is often just master-catchup](../learnings/1784420055611-approver-human-agreement-full-chain-vindication-bl.md)).

**Regression / self-contradiction BLOCK catches — the strongest 🔴s need no external corroboration.** A failing test the PR *itself adds* (its FileCheck oracle vs its own emitted output) is self-contradicting: either the code is wrong or the spec-of-record is wrong, and it is the strongest possible 🔴 — do NOT downgrade to ABSTAIN just because a relaxed `ci_green_on_sha` clause "passes" (clause-pass governs eligibility; CI red is challenger *evidence*, never laundered by a clause that doesn't require CI) ([[approver/challenger] PR's own new feature test failing at head is a self-contradicting RED_BUG](../learnings/1784431283917-approver-challenger-pr-s-own-new-feature-test-fail.md)). A **pre-existing untouched** test failing at the pinned head is a regression even without a master baseline when three conditions hold — the test predates the PR, the PR doesn't modify it, and the diff touches exactly the path the test exercises ([[approver/calibration] pre-existing-untouched-test-failing-at-head-is-regression-even-without-base-green](../learnings/1784420632496-approver-calibration-pre-existing-untouched-test-f.md)); when you CAN reach master, the airtight three-part proof is untouched-in-diff + present-and-failing-at-master? no + new-symbol-absent-on-master → provably green on master, red at head ([[approver/calibration] pre-existing untouched test regressing (green on master) = confirmed blast radius, not a flake](../learnings/1784431292789-approver-calibration-pre-existing-untouched-test-r.md)). A **tests-only** revision after a BLOCK can turn CI green while leaving a CI-invisible code 🔴 untouched — diff the code-file blobs; if byte-identical the revision did not address the code defect, re-verify the 🔴 stands and don't let now-green CI launder it ([[approver/challenger] tests-only revision can turn CI green without touching the buggy code — diff the code blob, don't trust green](../learnings/1784436534792-approver-challenger-tests-only-revision-can-turn-c.md)). The most dangerous variant: a test WEAKENED to green a *reproduced* failure on the exact code under review. slangpy#1075 finally added the reviewer-requested 33-texture batched-upload regression test that FAILED deterministically on Metal (both Release+Debug, real texel mismatches `-93 == 0`) while Linux/Windows passed; the very next push, titled "Make batched upload regression portable", greened CI by DELETING the mip loop so it compared only mip-0 (the directly-uploaded base data that matched) — the generated mips that actually diverged are no longer asserted. A naive "gap fixed + test added + CI green → approve" is a false-safe: the green was manufactured by narrowing the assertion to exclude exactly the thing that failed, on the path both Devin (🔴) and CodeRabbit (Major) flagged. Whenever a revision adds/edits a test on the reviewed path, diff the test itself (`gh api commits/<sha> --jq '.files[]|select(.filename|test("test"))|.patch'`) and ask: did the assertions get STRONGER (more coverage) or WEAKER (fewer levels/fields)? A `memcmp` narrowed to a subset right after that assertion failed for real is the tell — cross-reference the failing job log at the prior head, and a consistent 2-cell same-platform failure in the PR's OWN new test with real data mismatches is a reproduced regression, not a 1/12 timing flake ([[approver/false-safe] A test weakened to green a REPRODUCED failure on the code under review is a hard no-approve signal — read the test diff, not just the green](../learnings/1785493071728-approver-false-safe-a-test-weakened-to-green-a-rep.md)). Beyond the diff: a narrow-looking change to a **shared per-modifier/per-attribute dispatch arm** has blast radius = union of ALL producers of that modifier, not the syntax in the PR title — grep for every synthetic producer (compiler options like `-no-mangle`, desugaring, parser/checker stamping) before WOULD_APPROVE; a Step-0 recall-predicted-shape match confirms INTENT but says nothing about unintended collateral scope ([[approver/challenger] shared-modifier-lowering-arm-change-has-blast-radius-beyond-explicit-syntax](../learnings/1784420619202-approver-challenger-shared-modifier-lowering-arm-c.md)). A **visibility-*raising*** default change is dangerous exactly where a cap-check would newly fire — grep every reader that *compares* visibilities (`UseOfLessVisibleType`/E30604, ctor-synthesis) because a public struct with a less-visible field type that compiled at 2025 can newly hard-error at 2026; new green tests only cover shapes the author thought of ([[approver/challenger] visibility-elevation feature must probe the UseOfLessVisibleType cap-check](../learnings/1784417628061-approver-challenger-visibility-elevation-feature-m.md)). A **"simplify"/refactor** revision that preserves the headline fix can silently drop a DELIBERATE distinction the removed abstraction encoded — diff the removed type/helper's semantics, not just the target fix (an `OutputDestination{isStdout}`→plain-`String` collapse re-introduced a spurious diagnostic) ([[approver/challenger-miss] a "simplify"/refactor revision can preserve the headline fix while silently regressing a sibling correctness distinction](../learnings/1784381039011-approver-challenger-miss-a-simplify-refactor-revis.md)). And a fix that narrows behavior for a target class can create a **fresh doc 🔴** in the very `@remarks`/comment the PR added earlier — re-scan the revision's full non-code diff for prose still describing the pre-fix behavior; "old 🔴 resolved" and "no new 🔴" are two separate checks ([[approver/challenger] a fix that resolves the flagged bug can introduce a new one in its own doc/comment change](../learnings/1784440929327-approver-challenger-a-fix-that-resolves-the-flagge.md)).

**Devin's structured verdict is the only load-bearing Devin signal — prose and page-counters are not.** Devin's free-text summary can confidently describe features NOT in the diff (it absorbs the PR *description's* over-claims as if verified) — load-bear only on the structured `## Bugs`/`## Flags` counts, spot-check any capability claim against `gh pr diff`, and caveat the doc when prose is refuted ([[approver/challenger] devin-prose-unreliable-trust-only-structured-verdict](../learnings/1784390121609-approver-challenger-devin-prose-unreliable-trust-o.md)). Extend the same distrust to the page's `N Flag` header counter: it can read `1 Flag` while the parsed `## Flags` section says `(none reported)` — the structured section wins (it's what the synthesis parses); reconcile or annotate rather than propagate ([[approver/challenger] Devin page "N Flag" counter can disagree with the structured flags section — trust the structured verdict](../learnings/1784417635185-approver-challenger-devin-page-n-flag-counter-can-.md)).

**Don't take a CI red at face value — resolve what it represents.** `check-ci` is slang's aggregation/gating job (`jq` over the `needs` context, `exit 1` if any dependency ≠ success), so a `check-ci` failure is a downstream reflection of some other leg, never an independent signal — read its log for the named dependency and triage only the real leg; counting it double-counts one failure ([[approver/calibration] check-ci-is-aggregation-gate-not-independent-failure](../learnings/1784420509811-approver-calibration-check-ci-is-aggregation-gate-.md)). And a FileCheck failure whose "expected string" is English prose is usually a **test-authoring artifact**, not a code defect: LLVM FileCheck parses a literal `CHECK:` token *anywhere* on a line including backticked prose comments, registering a phantom directive that fires early and masks the real assertion — the fix is rewording the comment, the code was correct all along ([[approver/calibration] a stray `CHECK:` token inside a prose comment is a test-authoring failure that masquerades as a code defect](../learnings/1784436545771-approver-calibration-a-stray-check-token-inside-a-.md)).

**A behavior-inert build-warning fix clears on the affected green legs.** An "initialize-to-default to silence `-Werror=maybe-uninitialized`" PR looks like it could alter behavior, but when it sits behind a `SLANG_RETURN_ON_FAIL`-style result-returning helper that writes the out-param only on the success path and the caller returns on failure before reading it, the initializer is runtime-inert — the diagnostic is a genuine `-Og` data-flow false-positive across the helper-call boundary. Verify (a) the initializer is a valid existing enum member and (b) the helper's write-gate; then green on the exact affected legs (esp. the one that failed pre-fix) is sufficient challenger evidence, because the only realistic failure is a CI-visible build break, not a silent miscompile. Classify protected paths from the changed-file list, not a misleading branch name ([[approver/calibration] uninit-var -Og -Werror fix behind result-returning helper is behavior-inert](../learnings/1784389774470-approver-calibration-uninit-var-og-werror-fix-behi.md)).

**A recalled fix-shape prediction is the strongest challenger prior — but a checklist to VERIFY, not a substitute.** When Step-0 recall surfaces a prior triage of the SAME issue the PR fixes, the recalled fix prediction becomes a concrete axis-by-axis diff checklist against source at the pinned head — the match (plus clean clauses + head-current Devin + green CI legs) is what justifies WOULD_APPROVE on a thin tier; a DEVIATION from the prediction is exactly the signal recall exists to surface ([[approver/calibration] recall-predicted-fix-shape-verify-diff-matches-prediction](../learnings/1784417192285-approver-calibration-recall-predicted-fix-shape-ve.md)).

**Human-agreement calibration — split merge provenance before scoring a `pr_merged` join.** The `record_human_verdict` mapping stamps APPROVED on any merge, but the *signal strength* depends on WHO merged: an **independent-maintainer APPROVED same head** (reviewer ≠ author) is STRONG agreement, whereas an **author self-merge** (or a merge with only bot+author COMMENTED) is WEAK — it attests "the author was confident," not "an independent reviewer weighed your concern," especially when the concern was CI-invisible and shadow-mode never posted it. Pull `mergedBy` + `reviewDecision` + `reviews[].state/commit.oid`, never just `merged=true` ([[approver/human-agreement] author self-merge with no independent review is a weak agreement signal](../learnings/1784392147522-approver-human-agreement-author-self-merge-with-no.md)). This corrected an over-conservative read of slang#12147: the first pass framed the self-merge as evidence the R5 ABSTAIN was "over-conservative," but a self-merge does NOT refute a withhold — do not "learn" to lower your bar from one ([[approver/human-agreement] CORRECTION to slang#12147 calibration: it was an AUTHOR SELF-MERGE (weak signal)](../learnings/1784386500014-approver-human-agreement-correction-to-slang-12147.md)); the calibration point that stands is to grade a false-**POSITIVE** (over-rejection) by TRIGGER-LIKELIHOOD × BLAST-RADIUS, reserving ABSTAIN for plausible-everyday-trigger false-positives or any false-**NEGATIVE** ([[approver/human-disagreement] ABSTAIN over a pathological-only false-positive diagnostic; maintainer merged with it](../learnings/1784386401932-approver-human-disagreement-abstain-over-a-patholo.md), [[approver/human-agreement] author self-merge is a WEAK 'APPROVED' signal — a shadow-mode ABSTAIN … is vindicated (not refuted)](../learnings/1784386435249-approver-human-agreement-author-self-merge-is-a-we.md)). The strong-agreement archetype landed on slang#12133: WOULD_APPROVE @R4 == merged head, jkwak-work gave an explicit APPROVED and merged the same SHA — a 4-revision chain of known-shape deltas whose prior verified safety carried forward every time ([[approver/human-agreement] slang#12133 MERGED-agreement vindicated across 4 revisions](../learnings/1784391254624-approver-human-agreement-slang-12133-merged-agreem.md)). The safe-direction DISAGREEMENT archetype landed on slang#11471: a maintainer merged over two shadow BLOCK 🔴s (an example-only assert + an untrusted-input-only DoS) — vindicated on correctness (the eventual production review confirmed both verbatim), the disagreement was on blocking-*threshold* (blast-radius/code-location/threat-model), not correctness; annotate that severity axis so the join reads as expected, don't soften the BLOCK ([[approver/human-disagreement] Maintainer merged over a production-review 🔴 (example-only assert + untrusted-input DoS)](../learnings/1784391272446-approver-human-disagreement-maintainer-merged-over.md)).


## Contradictions / supersessions

- A refuted 🔴 does NOT round up to WOULD_APPROVE (shadow-mode never rounds up a doc's 🔴) — it lands `ABSTAIN_POLICY:CHALLENGER_CONCERN`. ⚠️The slang-rhi#800 citation here is **corrected**: that 🔴 was never cleanly refuted (NOT-BLOCK **but NOT-CLEARED**), so it exemplifies the *withhold*, not the refutation ([[approver/challenger-miss-averted] Metal direct-encoder-operand — CORRECTED 08-03](../learnings/1784338866210-approver-challenger-miss-averted-metal-direct-enco.md)); symmetrically a same-head human APPROVAL does not flip an independent OPEN_GAP up ([[approver/human-agreement] A same-head human APPROVAL is a JOIN signal, not a reason to flip your OPEN_GAP to WOULD_APPROVE](../learnings/1784281316765-approver-human-agreement-a-same-head-human-approva.md)).
- ⚠️ **WITHDRAWN — the slang-rhi#800 residency corollary.** The sentence *"the `!m_hasResidencySet` **fallback** merged unverified — only a `SLANG_RHI_METAL_NO_RESIDENCY_SET` run closes it"* (and its #801 sibling *"the map is dead code because `m_hasResidencySet` is true on Apple-Silicon = these legs"*) is **withdrawn on both PRs**, everywhere it appeared on this page — the false-positive-refutation section, the #801 masked-test paragraph, this list, and the footer descriptions. Truth: `m_hasResidencySet` is set only under `supportsFamily(MTL::GPUFamilyApple6)`, the hosted Paravirtual adapter lacks Apple6, ⇒ **CI runs the FALLBACK by default**; the residency-SET path is the uncovered one and the missing artifact is an **Apple6 run**, never the env var. The two rows were *mutually inconsistent* (adjacent PRs, same subsystem, opposite defaults for one flag) — that inconsistency was the tell, and a 30-second grep of one's own adjacent notes would have exposed it before the join did ([[approver/calibration] a caveat can be wrong toward EXCESS caution — cross-check sibling rows before filing](../learnings/1785778241162-approver-calibration-a-caveat-can-be-wrong-in-the-.md), [[approver/challenger-miss] the fallback fact, inverted in my own record on #801](../learnings/1785778085689-approver-challenger-miss-a-diagnostic-captured-onl.md), [[approver/calibration] clause (a) of the #801 root-cause paragraph is FALSE](../learnings/1785776756858-approver-calibration-closing-a-coverage-gap-can-un.md)). Neither decision changed (both stay ABSTAIN, both merged, both joins recorded APPROVED) — only the **basis**, which is the part future decisions reuse. **Meta-rule this establishes:** a correction filed as a NEW atom does **not** disarm the stale one. Only an edit that *names the file* and *quotes the superseded sentence* stops the next reader mining the old wording — so sweep for the SUPERSEDED phrasing (headings → title/frontmatter → tables → index rows → prose → serialized ledger payloads), never for your new phrasing, and re-record the `approval_decisions` row when the retraction touches its `challenger`/`clauses` field ([[approver/challenger-miss] the retraction's blast radius includes the RECORDED LEDGER ROW](../learnings/1785785616042-approver-challenger-miss-n-sources-agree-is-a-clai.md)).
- A verified 🔴 is NEVER downgraded to WOULD_APPROVE by an author self-merge — a self-merge is a WEAK signal, not a refutation ([[approver/human-agreement] CORRECTION to slang#12147 calibration: it was an AUTHOR SELF-MERGE (weak signal)](../learnings/1784386500014-approver-human-agreement-correction-to-slang-12147.md), superseding the "maintainer merged ⇒ my ABSTAIN was over-conservative" framing of [[approver/human-disagreement] ABSTAIN over a pathological-only false-positive diagnostic](../learnings/1784386401932-approver-human-disagreement-abstain-over-a-patholo.md)).
- A DISMISSED human review is NOT a standing CHANGES_REQUESTED — it does not cap at ABSTAIN, whereas an unresolved CHANGES_REQUESTED does ([[approver/challenger] module-version-bump synchronize (k_maxSupportedModuleVersion): audit value/lockstep/additivity against the design docs; DISMISSED human review is not a CHANGES_REQUESTED veto — slang#12133 R3](../learnings/1784343540068-approver-challenger-module-version-bump-synchroniz.md) vs [[approver/challenger] standing human CHANGES_REQUESTED vetoes WOULD_APPROVE regardless of clean doc](../learnings/1784326394685-approver-challenger-standing-human-changes-request.md)).
- "Green CI ⇒ safe" holds ONLY for CI-catchable failure classes (build breaks); for codegen/logic/miscompile PRs green CI is blind, and combined-status is non-causal in both directions.
- The #12141 full-arc write-up is **corrected on three points** by its own follow-up: (1) closure was PARTIAL — the author fixed the suppression-bypass (child-sink inherits the parent's `m_severityOverrides`) but RETAINED the over-broad forwarding (now suppression-aware), so it is not "the gap was fixed exactly as next-action"; (2) the `-Wno-31200` `NOWARNCHECK` test lives in commit `17bf5c1924b3`, not `4f4140d4`; (3) an R3 ABSTAIN_POLICY/OPEN_GAP row is EXCLUDED from agreement scoring — the correct framing is "full-arc vindication of DETECTION + REMEDIATION," never "three-row agreement." Trust the correction [[approver/human-agreement] CORRECTION to full-arc-vindication #12141: PARTIAL gap closure (suppression-bypass fixed, broad-forwarding retained), not "whole gap fixed" or "end-to-end agreement"](../learnings/1784838665378-approver-human-agreement-correction-to-full-arc-vi.md) where it conflicts with the initial account [[approver/human-agreement] full-arc vindication: R3 ABSTAIN(OPEN_GAP) merged only AFTER the author implemented the exact next-action (copy sink severity-overrides + add -Wno test); BLOCK→BLOCK→ABSTAIN→merge](../learnings/1784838040864-approver-human-agreement-full-arc-vindication-r3-a.md); the meta-lesson is that celebratory "vindicated" write-ups drift into overclaiming — scope the vindication to the specific concern addressed and re-verify commit/test attribution against gh.
