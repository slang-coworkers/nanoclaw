# CI Sweep Ranking and Honest Counting

How a red set gets *counted and ranked* — not classified — without publishing a plausible, self-consistent, un-contradicted number that points a maintainer at the wrong bucket. Companion to [CI Runners & Flake Triage part 4](ci-runners-flake-triage-4.md) (sweep mechanics, dedup, and reporting).

## TL;DR

- **Name a ranking's UNIT before headlining a superlative** — frequency and blast radius invert. On `test-windows-*-gpu / test-slang`, RPC breakdowns were ~9× more *frequent* than GPU device loss but device loss was ~40× more *damaging per event*; `slang-test` retries individual tests but past a ceiling promotes every pending failure straight to terminal, so a device loss (~100 tests) blows the net and loses the retry, while an RPC drop (1–4) stays under and is retried.
- **Check the DENOMINATOR is the same population before comparing two rates.** A shared job-name prefix (`test-falcor /`) is not one population — mixing `Test (Falcor)` with `Test (Falcor Perf)` diluted a rate ~2× and nearly published a "4.5× off-runner gap" that was actually no gap. Bound the unclassifiable with a floor/ceiling; if the conclusion flips between them you have none. A ratio between your number and someone else's is the highest-risk claim you can make.
- **Never rank flakes by grepping your own log's free-text field.** A per-sweep phrase *you* compose is an artifact, not a CI signal — a 7-day grep for `falcor` inflated to 41 hits / 27 PRs because 17 carried the author's own bulk stale-backlog label; the genuine bucket was 9 hits / 5 PRs. Enumerate the distinct `check` values, rank on the CI-emitted axis, print `last=<ts>` beside every count, rank by declines not reruns-fired.
- **A zero-action sweep needs a basis outside your own ledger.** 0 reruns is the most comfortable outcome and is contradicted by nothing — rank the repo-wide cross-section. `action_required` is an approval gate not a failure (`--failed` can't clear it); a `cancelled` job hitting its ceiling exactly is a cost regression (only the arithmetic discriminates); a run can sit `queued` for months. Name a basis that predicts the same zero, or you measured your own inactivity.
- **A correction's verdict and its mechanism have different shelf lives — reconcile the mechanism's UNIT before banking it.** A draft cascade multiplies JOBS inside one run, but a skip/cancel figure counts RUNS; adopt the verdict, then check the mechanism divides the number you quoted — a real mechanism one unit down passes every plausibility check while explaining only a fraction.

## Ranking a Sweep Honestly: Unit, Denominator, and an Independent Basis (2026-08-08/09 fold)

Five failures from two slang CI sweeps, all in how a red set gets *counted and ranked* rather than classified — each yields a plausible, self-consistent, un-contradicted number pointing a maintainer at the wrong bucket.

**Name a ranking's UNIT; frequency and blast radius invert.** On `test-windows-*-gpu / test-slang`, RPC breakdowns occurred 18× vs GPU device loss 2× (~9× more frequent), but device loss redded ~200 terminal tests vs RPC's ~18–72 (~40× more damaging *per event*). Mechanism, in both logs as `Too many failed tests for retry(110) - setting all to failed`: `slang-test` retries individual `failed(pending retry)` tests but past a ceiling promotes every pending failure straight to terminal — a device loss (~100 tests) blows the net and **loses the retry meant to absorb transient GPU faults**, an RPC drop (1–4) stays under and is retried. The bare superlative "single largest failure mode" shipped with the unit-naming rule already in-store — holding a rule is not applying it ([a CI failure ranking inverts by unit](../learnings/1786238081011-a-ci-failure-ranking-inverts-by-unit-frequency-vs-.md)).

**Check the DENOMINATOR is the same population before comparing two rates.** A flake's 6.3% CI rate vs a maintainer's local ~17% nearly published "4.5× higher off-runner" — the 430 "tested" legs mixed `Test (Falcor)` (203, runs the image tests) with `Test (Falcor Perf)` (227, cannot hit this test), diluting ~2×; against the correct 203 the AV rate is 7.9% (ceiling 12.8%), which brackets local ~16.7% too closely to assert any gap — the answer flipped to "no." Name the population before dividing (a shared `test-falcor /` prefix is *not* one population), **bound the unclassifiable with a floor/ceiling** (if the conclusion flips between them you have none), and a ratio between your number and someone else's is the highest-risk claim you can make ([check the denominator is the same population](../learnings/1786222540253-before-comparing-two-failure-rates-check-the-denom.md)).

**Never rank flakes by grepping your own log's free-text field.** A 7-day grep for `falcor` returned 41 hits / 27 PRs — reads "escalate falcor." Enumerating the distinct `check` values (the tell) showed 17 carried `check == "CI / Falcor / formatting (aged)"`, the author's *own* bulk stale-backlog label on PRs where no falcor job failed; excluding it, the genuine GBuffer/falcor bucket was 9 hits / 5 PRs, last fired 08-07 (5th, not active). The inflated number is plausible and passes every sum check — only its *membership* is wrong. `Counter(str(r.get("check")) for r in hits).most_common()`; a per-sweep phrase *you* compose is an artifact — rank on the CI axis, print `last=<ts>`, rank by declines not reruns-fired ([never grep your own log's reason field to rank flakes](../learnings/1786227400254-never-grep-your-own-log-s-reason-field-to-rank-fla.md)).

**A zero-action sweep needs a basis outside your own ledger.** 76 PRs, 22 red, 0 reruns — the most comfortable outcome, contradicted by nothing. Rank the repo-wide 24h cross-section (614 runs, `got >= total_count`, `status` before `conclusion`: 378 success / 176 skipped / 48 cancelled / 11 failure / 1 nonterminal); of the 11, 9 were `wait-for-human-priority` yields (designed backpressure), 1 the master nightly, 1 a known regression. Three reds that look rerunnable and are not: **`action_required` is an approval gate, not a failure** (`--failed` cannot clear it); **a `cancelled` job hitting its ceiling exactly is a cost regression** (#12354's 4 cancels hit ceilings 30/50/50/80 min at four distinct stamps — only the arithmetic discriminates, log says `The operation was canceled.` for all); a run can sit `queued` for 75 days. Name a basis outside your action log that predicts the same zero, or you measured your own inactivity ([a zero-action CI sweep needs an independent basis](../learnings/1786242220764-a-zero-action-ci-sweep-needs-an-independent-basis-.md)).

**A correction's verdict and mechanism have different shelf lives — reconcile the mechanism's UNIT.** "176 skipped + 48 cancelled runs vs 11 failures" as a cost figure was correctly killed as `conclusion`-as-cause (draft confound: `ci.yml:15` skips a draft's run + cascades ~40 jobs). Verdict right — but the cascade multiplies JOBS inside one run, and 176/48 count *runs*: `ci.yml` is only 16 of 176 skips (9%, intra-run), the other 160 are GPU-less workflows (`claude.yml` 55 trigger-guard no-ops), cancels dominated by `pr-ci-complete.yml` (36) plumbing. Adopt the verdict, then name the unit the mechanism acts on and check it divides the number you quoted — a real mechanism one unit down passes every plausibility check while explaining a fraction ([reconcile the unit of a confound before banking its mechanism](../learnings/1786242578317-reconcile-the-unit-of-a-confound-before-banking-it.md)).
