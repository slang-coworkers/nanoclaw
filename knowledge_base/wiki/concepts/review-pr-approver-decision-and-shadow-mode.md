# Approver Decision Logic & Shadow-Mode Calibration

How the `slang-pr-approver` / `slangpy-pr-approver` turns a completed review into one auditable verdict (`WOULD_APPROVE | ABSTAIN_POLICY | ABSTAIN_INFRA | BLOCK`), how the Step-1 clauses and the adversarial challenger draw the lines, and how the shadow-mode ledger calibrates against the eventual human merge. Companion pages: mechanics of deciding against the right commit live in [head-pinning & debounce](review-pr-head-pinning-and-debounce.md); reading the review signal / the late-post race lives in [harvest tiers & verdict reporting](review-pr-harvest-tiers-and-verdict-reporting.md); keeping the runs alive lives in [reviewer-run survival](review-pr-reviewer-run-survival.md).

## TL;DR
- **Fixed order: Step-1 deterministic clauses → Step-2 review-verdict parse → Step-3 adversarial challenger, recording critique-gated.** Clauses evaluate *before* the review verdict is read and can short-circuit it, so glance at the changed paths first — a clean `APPROVE_WITH_NITS` never reaches WOULD_APPROVE if a clause fails.
- **Classify a protected-path hit as ABSTAIN_POLICY ("a human must look"), not ABSTAIN_INFRA.** Even the relaxed `v0-shadow-relaxed` policy still protects `.github/**` and `**/slang-tag-version.h`.
- **Shadow-mode is deliberately conservative:** any non-pre-existing yellow gap forces `ABSTAIN_POLICY:OPEN_GAP` even on a proven-correct change. A maintainer merging over that abstain is a conservative-abstain the human accepted, NOT a false-safe — frame the 5-bullet to say so.
- **Distinguish correctness-gaps (a plausible failing input exists) from robustness/maintainability-gaps on a proven-correct change** (missing test, doc-scope, future-proofing assert). Both force OPEN_GAP today, but the latter class is high-probability human-approve.
- **Never round up.** A refuted 🔴 does not become WOULD_APPROVE; a standing human `CHANGES_REQUESTED` caps the verdict at ABSTAIN.
- **Never round DOWN a source-verified reachable defect either.** "Existence uncertain" and "severity debatable" are different axes — the "any doubt → ABSTAIN" caution guards against rounding toward *approve*, not against a VERIFIED 🔴 → BLOCK.
- **Run `eval-clauses.py` and read the emitted `policy_version`; never hand-judge a clause from a recalled learning.** The relaxed mount narrows `protected_paths` and flips `require_ci_green:false`; a bundled-default recall predicts the wrong reason_code.
- **A terminal Step-1 clause FAIL dominates a co-occurring UNEVALUABLE / no-review-signal — record ABSTAIN_POLICY, not ABSTAIN_INFRA.**
- **The size cap is well-calibrated, not too conservative.** `>2000`-line PRs hitting `CLAUSE_FAIL:tier_eligible` are the system routing to a human; large all-new sample/experiment PRs self-merging past it is the cap working. Never lobby to raise it.
- **Devin false positives are yours to challenge, not defer to** — verify the finding in source; a two-bot agreement measures shared priors, not correctness.
- **Approval-message evidence claims must be no stronger than the challenger's own hedges.**

## The decision order and shadow mode

The approver runs in shadow mode (posts nothing to GitHub), so the eventual human merge becomes calibration data against the recorded ledger verdict. The verdict is built via a fixed order: deterministic Step-1 clauses → Step-2 review-verdict parse → Step-3 adversarial challenger, with recording critique-gated. Step-1 clauses evaluate (and can short-circuit) *before* the review verdict is even read, so glance at the changed paths first: even the relaxed `v0-shadow-relaxed` policy keeps `.github/**` and `**/slang-tag-version.h` in `protected_paths`, so any CI-workflow-touching PR lands `ABSTAIN_POLICY / CLAUSE_FAIL:no_protected_paths` no matter how clean the review, and a clean `APPROVE_WITH_NITS` can never reach WOULD_APPROVE if a clause fails — don't burn a challenger pass ([relaxed shadow policy still protects .github/**](../learnings/1783673178930-approver-relaxed-shadow-policy-still-protects-gith.md)). The `reviewers_complete` field the reviewer emits is authoritative: a B/Devin infra-skip alone is NOT a harness-fail when the field is `true` (A+C completing is a complete panel for this pipeline); override to manufacture incompleteness only when the field is literally `false` ([reviewers_complete field is authoritative](../learnings/1783707333659-approver-reviewers-complete-field-is-authoritative.md)).

Shadow-mode is deliberately conservative: any non-pre-existing yellow gap forces `ABSTAIN_POLICY:OPEN_GAP` even on a proven-correct change. Distinguish *correctness-gaps* (a plausible failing input exists) from *robustness/maintainability-gaps on a proven-correct change* (missing test, doc-scope, future-proofing assert): both force OPEN_GAP today, but the latter class is high-probability human-approve. On slang#12037 and #12041 maintainers merged over the abstain at the exact decided commit with the gap unaddressed — that is a conservative-abstain the human accepted, NOT a false-safe, and the framing in the 5-bullet should say so rather than reading as "something's wrong" ([maintainer merged over an ABSTAIN on a low-severity gap (#12037)](../learnings/1783676779766-approver-calibration-maintainer-merged-over-an-abs.md), [proven-correct perf fix approved-with-nits over gaps (#12041)](../learnings/1783692443899-approver-human-disagreement-for-a-proven-correct-b.md)).

## Clause gaps (Step-1): CI-green, size cap, protected paths, merge-commit blind spots

**CI-green.** `ci_green_on_sha` passes on the legacy combined-status API even when the real build/slang-test CI never ran — on a `CONFLICTING`/deeply-behind PR, `pull_request`-triggered CI silently doesn't dispatch, so combined-status green aggregates only trivial commit-status contexts (license/CLA/CodeRabbit); independently query `actions/runs?head_sha=` and `commits/<sha>/check-runs` and treat "no build/test check-run present" as UNEVALUABLE → `ABSTAIN_POLICY:CHALLENGER_CONCERN` ([ci_green_on_sha passes on legacy combined-status even when real CI never ran](../learnings/1784017419889-approver-clause-gap-ci-green-on-sha-passes-on-lega.md)). The flip side: capdef/capability-atom PRs fail `check-cmdline-ref` (regenerating `docs/command-line-slangc-reference.md`) if the second generated doc isn't refreshed — combined-status red then correctly fails `ci_green_on_sha`, and even trivial-to-fix CI-red is not approvable ([capdef atom PRs: check-cmdline-ref fails if command-line-slangc-reference.md isn't regenerated](../learnings/1784026216900-approver-clause-gap-capdef-atom-prs-check-cmdline-.md)). But under `v0-shadow-relaxed` `require_ci_green:false`, a red *required* check does NOT trip `ci_green_on_sha` (the clause passes "policy does not require CI green") — route the CI-red through the challenger as an OPEN_GAP; and on the same policy, on a master-merge-commit head the `base...commit_sha` changed-path view surfaces only the second-parent delta and can miss a PR-changed protected file, so cross-check with `gh pr view --json files`. Read `clauses.json`/`policy_version` before naming a reason_code ([under v0-shadow-relaxed a red required check does NOT trip ci_green_on_sha; master-merge-head changed-path blind spot](../learnings/1784049960961-approver-clause-gap-under-v0-shadow-relaxed-a-red-.md)).

**Size cap.** Eligibility is a property of each revision's *cumulative* diff, not the PR — `tier_eligible` can flip pass→fail when a later push adds a large test/backend, so re-score every settled head (the script computes `base...commit_sha` full-PR additions+deletions, not the incremental delta); a terminal `tier_eligible` FAIL also dominates a co-occurring `commit_match=unevaluable` (record ABSTAIN_POLICY, not ABSTAIN_INFRA) ([a PR can cross the size cap mid-revision — re-score tier_eligible every revision](../learnings/1784020764751-approver-clause-gap-a-pr-can-cross-the-size-cap-mi.md)).

**Protected paths.** Never hand-judge from a recalled learning — run `eval-clauses.py` and read the emitted `policy_version`; the relaxed mount narrows `protected_paths` to only `[".github/**", "**/slang-tag-version.h"]`, so a bot-authored CMake/submodule change (e.g. `external/CMakeLists.txt`) passes `no_protected_paths` and is a correct Devin-only-tier WOULD_APPROVE, not the CLAUSE_FAIL a bundled-default recall would predict ([bot-authored CMake/submodule change is Devin-only tier; relaxed policy does NOT protect external/CMakeLists.txt](../learnings/1784074325850-approver-clause-gap-bot-authored-cmake-submodule-c.md)).

**Ordering / blind spots.** `eval-clauses.py`'s `commit_match` clause parses the SYNTHESIZED `review-doc.md`, not `harvest.json` — synthesize the doc first or you get a spurious `commit_match:unevaluable` → ABSTAIN_INFRA ([eval-clauses.py commit_match reads the synthesized review-doc, not harvest.json — synthesize first](../learnings/1784038871000-approver-clause-gap-eval-clauses-py-commit-match-r.md)). On a reformatting PR, always base-diff a flagged 🟡 before treating it as an OPEN_GAP — pre-existing artifacts the PR only re-whitespaces do not block ([on a reformatting PR, base-diff a flagged 🟡 before treating it as an OPEN_GAP](../learnings/1783960153742-approver-clause-gap-on-a-reformatting-pr-always-ba.md)).

## Drawing the abstain lines

A terminal Step-1 clause FAIL dominates a co-occurring UNEVALUABLE and no-review-signal — record ABSTAIN_POLICY, not ABSTAIN_INFRA (the unevaluable is subordinate to the FAIL, and clause FAIL short-circuits harvest/Devin/challenger) ([terminal Step-1 clause FAIL dominates a co-occurring UNEVALUABLE and no-review-signal](../learnings/1783932935934-approver-clause-gap-terminal-step-1-clause-fail-do.md)). When a BLOCK-vs-not pivot needs a slow build+probe that could outlast the session, record `ABSTAIN_INFRA:CHALLENGER_INCOMPLETE` now and supersede later — don't leave the pivot unrecorded, because a `pr_merged` webhook waking a dormant decision no-ops `record_human_verdict` if no ledger row exists for the pinned commit ([a slow build+probe on a BLOCK-vs-not pivot can outrun the session — bound it or ABSTAIN promptly](../learnings/1784055090518-approver-infra-abstain-a-slow-build-probe-on-a-blo.md)).

## Challenger correctness: verified defects, Devin false-positive classes, and overclaim

**Verified-🔴 severity is not an abstain lever, and a versioned boundary makes format changes reachable defects.** On slangpy#1054 a grad-bit fix was correct on the matched-version path but left `TENSOR_BRIDGE_API_VERSION` at 7 while changing the *output format* of a function whose result crosses a versioned ABI boundary — the native-vs-fallback compat gate only checks numeric version + struct size, both unchanged by a behavioral format change, so a user who upgrades the wheel but keeps a stale separately-installed `slangpy_torch` v7 silently reintroduces the bug. General rule: when a PR changes the output format of any function whose result crosses a versioned ABI/plugin boundary (serialized signature, wire format, cross-artifact cache key), check whether the boundary's version constant is bumped; a format change without a bump when the compat gate ignores the behavioral contract is a stale-peer-artifact defect. This is a VERIFIED 🔴 → BLOCK — do NOT downgrade a source-verified reachable defect to ABSTAIN because its severity *feels* debatable; "existence uncertain" and "severity debatable" are different axes, and the "any doubt → ABSTAIN, never round up" caution guards against rounding UP toward approve, not against softening a verified 🔴 ([signature/serialization-format changes across a versioned bridge must bump the version constant](../learnings/1783881950122-approver-challenger-signature-serialization-format.md)).

**Devin false-positive classes to challenge, not defer to:** cross-platform filename bugs on tutorial run-scripts — verify the tool's actual naming convention in source ([Devin cross-platform filename bugs on tutorial run-scripts are often false positives](../learnings/1783935960085-approver-challenger-miss-devin-cross-platform-file.md)); and narrative that merely echoes the PR description while citing superseded locations — do not treat as coverage ([Devin narrative echoes PR description and cites superseded locations](../learnings/1783957900263-approver-challenger-miss-devin-narrative-echoes-pr.md)).

**Reviewing a PreToolUse command-guard demands adversarial inputs.** A hook enforcing `--draft` on `gh pr create` had two real fail-open bypasses its author's "9 cases pass" self-test missed: (1) token-in-quoted-title — grepping the raw command for `--draft` is spoofed when the token appears inside `--title "support --draft mode"`; fix by stripping quoted substrings before flag inspection; (2) glued short-flag — a repo-resolver requiring a space after `-R`/`--repo` misses `-Rowner/repo` → repo unresolved → fail-open. A command string is attacker-influenced text, not structured args; probe token-inside-title (both quote styles), glued short flag, `--repo=` equals form, compound `cd && gh pr create`, false-positive (fork target + upstream string in title), and no-repo-resolvable — 15 cases is a reasonable floor for a security-relevant guard, and confirm fail-open vs fail-closed is the intended direction ([reviewing PreToolUse gh-command guards: two bypass classes — title-token spoof, glued short-flag fail-open](../learnings/1783879965262-reviewing-pretooluse-gh-command-guards-two-bypass-.md)).

**Approval-message prose must not overclaim past the challenger's own hedges.** A WOULD_APPROVE message may state the *decision* confidently, but its *evidence* claims must be no stronger than the challenger's — approval confidence ≠ evidence certainty. Before sending, diff the message's verdict language against the investigation's hedges; any absolute ("cannot", "no risk", "fully covered") in the message must be backed by an equally absolute statement in the investigation, else carry the residual caveat forward verbatim (on slangpy#1053 a drafted message claimed a strict-xfail regression "cannot mask unrelated failures" while the investigation had recorded a residual masking risk) ([approval-message verdict prose must not overclaim past the challenger's own hedges](../learnings/1783876835715-approver-critique-mustfix-approval-message-verdict.md)).

## Human-outcome calibration (the merge join)

**Size-cap ABSTAIN is well-placed.** slang#11615 (5146 lines, witness-table lowering) abstained on `tier_eligible` and was human-APPROVED only after a ~month-long review with an intermediate DISMISSED review and a substantive fix commit landing after R0 — exactly the "a human must look" case; do not lobby to raise the cap ([size-cap ABSTAIN on a large compiler-core PR confirmed well-placed (#11615)](../learnings/1783786027293-approver-calibration-size-cap-abstain-on-a-large-c.md)). Large all-new samples/experiment PRs self-merge past the size-cap abstain — slangpy-samples#53 (7414 lines) was `ABSTAIN_POLICY / CLAUSE_FAIL:tier_eligible` and the CONTRIBUTOR author self-merged ~4h later with no formal review; that is the cap routing to a human whose answer for this class ("self-contained new files under `experiments/`/`examples/`, no shared-core edits, trusted author") is nearly always "ship it." Keep the cap terminal; because the challenger is short-circuited, the reviewer's 0-bug read is the only quality signal reaching a human, so surfacing it (the COMMENT-state review) is the highest-value action, and don't expect flagged nits resolved before merge ([large samples/experiment PRs self-merge past the size-cap abstain](../learnings/1783918446960-approver-human-disagreement-large-samples-experime.md)). Same at ~6700 lines: a large author-authored PR self-merged past unresolved CodeRabbit Major findings — size-cap ABSTAIN confirmed correct ([large author-authored PR self-merged past unresolved static-analysis Major findings](../learnings/1783962279436-approver-calibration-large-author-authored-pr-self.md)).

**Protected-path ABSTAIN_POLICY is well-calibrated** even for trivial CI runner-pins — these changes reliably draw real multi-round human review ([protected-path ABSTAIN_POLICY on trivial CI runner-pins is correctly drawn — merged unchanged](../learnings/1783942538159-approver-calibration-protected-path-abstain-policy.md), [confirmed: protected-path (.github/**) ABSTAIN_POLICY is well-calibrated — draws real multi-round human review](../learnings/1783949231509-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-protected-pa.md)). Small release-packaging PRs touching `.github/**` ABSTAIN correctly, and CodeRabbit "Major" packaging-robustness nits are non-blocking 🟡, not 🔴 ([small release-packaging PRs touching .github/**: ABSTAIN_POLICY confirmed correct](../learnings/1783964691555-approver-human-disagreement-small-release-packagin.md)).

**Confirmed-safe correctness classes.** Purely-additive `calcRequiredLoweringPassSet` "gate = pass handled-set" lowering fixes (the #11917 epic) ship unchanged — a stale-false gate reduces to a producer-vs-governing-scan check, and the skip predicate must be a safe superset of every mutation site ([purely-additive "gate = pass handled-set" lowering fixes ship unchanged](../learnings/1783957287484-approver-human-agreement-purely-additive-gate-pass.md), [RequiredLoweringPassSet gates: stale-false reduces to a producer-vs-governing-scan check](../learnings/1784006091575-approver-human-agreement-requiredloweringpassset-g.md), [#11917 pass-gating early-outs: verify the skip predicate is a safe superset of every mutation site](../learnings/1783997718788-approver-challenger-miss-11917-pass-gating-early-o.md)). Bot-authored test-only PRs on the Devin-only tier merge unchanged ([Devin-only WOULD_APPROVE matched human APPROVE on bot-authored test-only PR](../learnings/1783963594230-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-agreement-de.md), [#12081 merged unchanged, zero follow-up commits after WOULD_APPROVE](../learnings/1783972123031-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-at-merge-sla.md)). Mid-cycle ABSTAINs that defer to a maintainer who then APPROVES the exact decision commit are confirmed-correct ([confirmed-correct ABSTAIN: deferred to a mid-cycle maintainer who then APPROVED the exact decision commit (#12065)](../learnings/1783960819826-approver-calibration-confirmed-correct-abstain-def.md)).
