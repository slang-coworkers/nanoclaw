# Head-Pinning, Debounce & Byte-Proving Under Live Head Churn

Deciding against the *right* commit when a PR head keeps moving. The single most recurring approver failure is deciding on a stale SHA — this page is the discipline that prevents it: pin the settled head, re-pin it after every slow phase and at both critique stages, debounce force-push bursts, and byte-prove that a frozen review still covers a moved head. Reading the *review signal* under the same churn (the late-post race, re-harvesting at record time) lives in [harvest tiers & verdict reporting](review-pr-harvest-tiers-and-verdict-reporting.md); the decision framework itself lives in [approver decision & shadow-mode](review-pr-approver-decision-and-shadow-mode.md).

## TL;DR
- **The subject of a decision is the compiled behavior, not a commit SHA.** Pin the settled head, then re-pin it — after the slow harvest/Devin phase AND at both critique stages (DECISION_REVIEW and OUTPUT_REVIEW). A stale pin is a guaranteed must-fix.
- **Pin with `--mode patch`** (diff vs merge-base, `diff_hash` = patch sha256, frozen), never `pr` mode (which binds the live tip and silently reviews a different commit after a mid-run synchronize).
- **A mid-iteration `diff_hash` mismatch is expected under debounce, not a bug.** Byte-prove: extract added non-comment `+` lines from both diffs and `diff` them — empty means identical, so the mismatch is comment-only/master-merge delta and the frozen review still covers the settled head.
- **The debounce window must outlast the burst.** A too-short poll (90 s, 15 min) desyncs the pinned SHA from live-head Devin/`gh pr diff`; size it *longer* than the observed inter-push cadence. A sustained force-push burst that starves every head-current decision is correctly `ABSTAIN_INFRA`, not an endless chase.
- **Never decide on the opening commit.** A clean single-file fix can grow a `.github/**` protected-path edit within ~20 min, flipping WOULD_APPROVE → ABSTAIN_POLICY.
- **On a synchronize you already decided, classify the delta cheaply** (`gh api compare/<last_approved>...<new_head>` + `gh pr diff | sha256sum`) rather than re-deriving from scratch.
- **A codegen-inert delta lets the standing decision ride** — but prove inertness by a FULL-file diff, not the commit message; a push *labeled* doc-only that touches emit/IR-lowering/eligibility/target-gating IS a codegen change.
- **A pure master-merge synchronize does NOT close a held coverage gap** — re-decide from the PR's own diff, not a maintainer's approve.
- **A single `gh pr view --json changedFiles` read within seconds of a synchronize can be transient.** Read scope 2–3× back-to-back; scope *shrinkage* is the direction that produces false-safes.
- **On a remediation revision, direct source verification is the load-bearing signal** — a source-verified fix cannot be flipped by a STALE Devin 🔴.

## Pin the settled head — and re-pin it

When a PR head keeps moving, do not chase it per-push. Pin the review to a *specific* commit with `--mode patch` (diff vs merge-base, `diff_hash` = patch sha256, frozen), never `pr` mode, which binds to the live tip and silently reviews a different commit after a mid-run synchronize ([pin a moving PR head with patch mode, not pr mode](../learnings/1783681496336-pin-a-moving-pr-head-with-patch-mode-not-pr-mode.md)). The subject of an approval decision is the **compiled behavior, not the commit SHA**, so re-pin the live head at BOTH critique stages, not just at build time — a rapidly-iterating author can push between build and record, and the codex gate re-verifies `headRefOid` at DECISION_REVIEW and OUTPUT_REVIEW, so a stale pin is a guaranteed must-fix ([re-pin live head at both critique stages](../learnings/1784037974102-approver-critique-mustfix-re-pin-live-head-at-both.md)). Re-pin the head *after* the slow harvest/Devin phase, not just at debounce start ([re-pin head AFTER slow harvest/Devin, not just at debounce start](../learnings/1783930401148-approver-critique-mustfix-re-pin-head-after-slow-h.md)), and re-run `gh pr view <pr> --json headRefOid,isDraft,state` and compare to the pinned `commit_sha` before `record_decision` — HEAD and the review snapshot can BOTH be behind; if the head moved, fetch the delta and check whether it touches the file(s) your finding rests on (orthogonal → keep pinned + add a Live-state note; touches it → fresh cycle), and note that a PR reverting to draft can RESOLVE a governance sub-point ([re-pin and re-check live PR head/draft state before recording — HEAD can move mid-run](../learnings/1783881964285-approver-critique-mustfix-re-pin-and-re-check-live.md)).

Never decide on the opening commit: on a fresh, actively-iterated PR the opening commit's diff is a poor predictor of the settled diff — a clean single-file fix grew into a `.github/**` protected-path edit within ~20 min, flipping WOULD_APPROVE to ABSTAIN_POLICY ([debounced settled head can expand scope into a protected path — never decide on the opening commit](../learnings/1783927363514-approver-clause-gap-debounced-settled-head-can-exp.md)). A synchronize burst can advance the head multiple times mid-wait: poll `headRefOid` on every re-harvest and re-pin to the settled head before harvesting, or you decide on a superseded SHA the primary never reviewed ([synchronize burst can advance head mid-wait — re-pin to settled head before harvest](../learnings/1784034617985-approver-challenger-miss-synchronize-burst-can-adv.md)).

## Debounce windows and force-push bursts

If pushes arrive faster than the ~25–40 min review turnaround, HOLD and arm a host-side quiescence poll whose window is *longer* than the observed inter-push cadence (30 min for ~20–25 min pushes) — a 15-min window fires mid-gap into more churn, and nobody is blocked when the PR is maintainer-authored ([when push cadence beats review turnaround, widen the quiescence window](../learnings/1783709416771-when-push-cadence-beats-review-turnaround-widen-th.md)). The window must outlast the burst: a too-short (90-second) poll desyncs the pinned SHA from live-head Devin/`gh pr diff` ([debounce window must outlast the burst — a too-short window desyncs the pinned SHA](../learnings/1783960363272-approver-critique-mustfix-debounce-window-must-out.md)). A sustained force-push burst (6 heads in ~85 min) starves every head-current decision — after a hard bound that is correctly `ABSTAIN_INFRA`, not an endless chase ([sustained force-push burst starves head-current decisions → ABSTAIN_INFRA](../learnings/1783958614926-approver-infra-abstain-sustained-force-push-burst-.md)). The general playbook for a head that keeps moving via synchronize/merge churn: debounce to a settled head, then classify and decide once ([reviewing a PR whose head keeps moving (synchronize/merge churn)](../learnings/1784006371550-reviewing-a-pr-whose-head-keeps-moving-synchronize.md)).

## Patch mode, byte-proving, and historical pinning

A frozen patch-mode review can validly cover a *moved* head if you byte-prove the code is identical: extract added non-comment `+` lines from both diffs and `diff` them (empty = identical); a `diff_hash` mismatch is then documented comment-only/master-merge delta, not a stale review — record the decision keyed to the settled head ([debounce live PR head churn, then byte-prove the frozen review still covers the settled head](../learnings/1783682287036-approver-reviewer-debounce-live-pr-head-churn-then.md)). For a *historical* R0-pinned review the R0 patch must apply onto the PR's historical base, not July master: repoint the clone's `origin` to a bare repo whose `master` == the R0 base commit — a local ref repoint alone is clobbered by the runner's startup `git fetch origin master` ([historical R0-pinned review: repoint clone origin to R0 base, not just local ref](../learnings/1783689995051-historical-r0-pinned-review-repoint-clone-origin-t.md)).

**Rebind PR-review-runner artifacts by `diff_hash`, not head — a clean rebase can leave the diff byte-identical.** When a fix-review request is re-dispatched after a rebase, do NOT decide "stale vs reusable" by head commit alone: the PR's own `gh pr diff` output (and its sha256) is what the reviewers actually consume, and a rebase that applies cleanly over an *unrelated* change to the same file leaves the PR's hunks and context lines unchanged → the unified diff is BYTE-IDENTICAL across the rebase even though the head moves (measured: head `1296bcf`→`c72356154a`, `sha256(diff)` stayed `462b33d5…`). Compute `gh pr diff <n> -R <repo> | sha256sum` and compare against each run's `pr-diff.reference` sha / the `diffhash12` embedded in the clarity run-dir name (`pr-pr<N>-<head>-<diffhash12>-…`); rebind by diff_hash, because a head-based salvage check wrongly discards a still-valid run. Reviewer C (clarity) and Devin key DIFFERENTLY: clarity is diff-content-bound (byte-identical diff ⇒ reusable), but Devin keys on head commit and auto-re-analyzes per head, so re-run Devin after a head change even when the diff is identical. Transcripts dirs are shared across ALL PRs and get reused/aged over a long-lived session — never assume a prior run's dir survives; list current dirs and match by the diff_hash in the name. Operational gotchas confirmed: `gh auth status` printing "token is invalid" is an App-token quirk (real `gh pr view/diff` still work — try the real op before declaring an outage); Reviewer A's `compose-and-run.sh` occasionally dies with `API Error: 400 … unexpected end of data` + `REVIEW-GUARD FAIL: zero Task/Agent subagent dispatches` (transient, not diff-size — retry once); dispatch runners via the harness `Bash(run_in_background=true)`, NOT manual `nohup … &` (a nohup child produced a 0-byte log and vanished); and `run-clarity.sh` takes flags directly (`--mode pr --pr N …`), never the skill-subcommand token as a positional arg. Review-content note: a pure-`out` param direct-write optimization doing `localVariable->replaceUsesWith(outputGlobal)` re-roots READS as well as writes — sound only because a pure `out` carries no defined incoming value (exactly why `in out` must keep its copy-in); that soundness invariant belongs in a code comment, not just the PR body ([rebind PR-review artifacts by diff_hash, not head — a clean rebase can leave the diff byte-identical](../learnings/1789183793058-rebind-pr-review-artifacts-by-diff-hash-not-head-a.md)).

## Re-deciding a synchronize / master-merge, and the codegen-inert shortcut

On a `synchronize` you already decided, classify the delta cheaply with `gh api compare/<last_approved>...<new_head>` and a `gh pr diff | sha256sum` match rather than re-deriving; the pre-existing exemption covers factual (not just cosmetic) findings the PR didn't touch ([re-decide a synchronize by diffing head vs your LAST-APPROVED SHA](../learnings/1783970228393-approver-clause-gap-re-decide-a-synchronize-by-dif.md)). For a master-merge-only branch, pin the PR-diff hash and re-verify structural claims when the base moves, treating pipeline-incompleteness as an explicit judgment ([master-merge churn: pin the PR-diff hash, re-verify structural claims when the base moves](../learnings/1784008035954-approver-human-agreement-master-merge-churn-pin-th.md)). When the protected-path edit is structural/invariant across revisions the ABSTAIN is deterministic — do NOT re-run every synchronize ([deterministic-ABSTAIN PRs — stop re-running on churn](../learnings/1783972579606-deterministic-abstain-prs-stop-re-running-on-churn.md)).

Do not burn the full gated cycle on every push. When a run of heads is **codegen-inert** — the `source/slang/**` diff (excluding tests, stripping pure-comment/blank/`SLANG_ASSERT` lines) is empty — let the standing recorded decision RIDE, logging the head as "covered by \<recorded-sha\> (codegen-inert delta)" rather than losing a race against the author every ~45 min ([standing decision rides codegen-inert synchronizes](../learnings/1784054163587-approver-clause-gap-standing-decision-rides-codege.md)). The guardrail is load-bearing: a push *labeled* doc-only that actually touches the emit path / IR-lowering / eligibility-predicate logic / target gating IS a codegen change and must re-trigger the full procedure (this is exactly where the `__grid_constant__` false-safe lived — verify behavior, never trust the commit message). At comment granularity, a comment/whitespace-only delta proven by a FULL-file diff (not the compare summary) carries a prior CLEAN logic verdict to the new SHA ([#11917 comment-only-delta re-verdict rode to merge with zero follow-up](../learnings/1784063797593-approver-calibration-11917-comment-only-delta-re-v.md)).

A **pure master-merge `synchronize` does NOT close a held coverage gap** — re-decide from the PR's own diff (`gh pr diff --name-only`; is the flagged file/test present at THIS head?), not from an orchestrator hint or a maintainer's approve; `diff_hash` legitimately differs across a master-merge even when the PR hunk is byte-identical because it hashes the full unified diff including context ([a pure master-merge synchronize does not close a held coverage gap](../learnings/1784077537602-approver-challenger-a-synchronize-that-is-a-pure-m.md)). And a single `gh pr view --json changedFiles` read within seconds of a synchronize can be transient (GitHub hasn't recomputed the merge base): read scope 2–3× back-to-back and require agreement — scope *shrinkage* below what a reviewer saw is the direction that produces false-safes, so a scope-larger critique finding is ground truth until you reproduce the smaller read ([a single gh pr view read of changedFiles can be transient during synchronize+force-push](../learnings/1784021958594-approver-challenger-miss-a-single-gh-pr-view-read-.md)).

## Re-deriving findings on a revision, and freshness on both sides

On a revision (`synchronize`) of a PR you previously BLOCKed, never trust "author addressed it": a fixup often targets only the loudest finding while another changed file stays byte-identical — `gh api .../compare/<prevHead>...<newHead>` to see which files moved, blob-compare each file that carried a prior finding, and re-derive every finding fresh against the new head (prior turns are context, one ledger row per commit) ([revision fixup can fix the flagged side while the other file's concern stays byte-identical](../learnings/1783801380207-approver-challenger-miss-revision-fixup-can-fix-th.md)). A still-present false claim the current revision's review didn't re-list is still a live gap — the challenger re-checks carried-over diff content, it doesn't defer to "not re-flagged" ([a still-present false claim the current revision's review didn't re-list is still a live gap](../learnings/1783937211976-approver-challenger-miss-a-still-present-false-cla.md)).

The dual freshness hazard: on a remediation revision, a STALE Devin snapshot re-flags the already-fixed finding and can drive a *false BLOCK* on the correct fix. Tells: line-number drift vs the new head, the narrative omitting the revision's headline change, `devin-commit-status.txt` reading "unknown" (not a positive "up to date"). Cross-check the finding's cited `file:line` against source at the pinned head; when stale, clear the browser profile and re-run, preserving the stale attempt for the audit trail. Direct source verification is the load-bearing signal on a remediation turn — Devin is corroboration, so a source-verified fix cannot be flipped by a stale 🔴 ([on a remediation revision, a STALE Devin snapshot re-flags the already-fixed finding — verify freshness or it drives a false BLOCK](../learnings/1783884242462-approver-challenger-miss-on-a-remediation-revision.md)).
