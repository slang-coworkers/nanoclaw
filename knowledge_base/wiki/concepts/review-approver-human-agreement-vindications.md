# PR-Approver: Human-Agreement Vindications & the Over-Conservative Calibration Record

The calibration record joins each shadow verdict against the human outcome (merged / approved / closed). This page collects the *vindications* — cases where a merge-at-exact-head with an independent approval confirmed the conservative-lean bar — and, just as important, the streak where the bar was measured **over-conservative** and the meta-errors that produced it (the slang-rhi import PRs and the #817 SRGB chain). It also carries the confirmed-by-merge archetype (DDS subresource-math).

## TL;DR

- **Merged-at-exact-head + independent (non-self) human approval = clean AGREEMENT.** Pull `mergedBy`/`author`/`reviewDecision`/`reviews[].state/commit.oid`; `gh pr view --json` has no `merged` field (use `state`/`mergedAt`/`mergedBy`).
- **A withhold resolved by a human is agreement, not a conservative miss.** An ABSTAIN whose gap ships unaddressed after merge confirms the gap was a reviewer note, not a blocker.
- **At n=3 consecutive losses with zero wins, the finding is about the BAR, not the PR.** Escalate the bar as a *policy question* — a self-loosened bar is invisible to the calibration record that detected the problem. Hash the files, don't trust the SHA (a sync between decision and approval may leave reviewed bytes unchanged).
- **Partition a count by the mechanism the fix addresses before using it as evidence for that fix.** If the partition is uneven, the count is not the evidence. n=1 cannot establish a rate — argue a policy change on principle.
- **An author's WIP declaration is evidence about who wants to look, not what they'd find.** Author intent belongs in the report's next-action, never in the verdict.
- **An abstain must price SEVERITY, not just uncertainty.** A verdict that totals only risks systematically under-approves — the fix's demonstrated benefit must go on the scale too. An approval that never saw the finding is not a refutation of it.
- **Replace "could a human usefully look at this?" (unfalsifiable) with "what outcome would prove this ABSTAIN WRONG?"** (names the observable before the decision). The falsifiable form is what converts a losing streak into a scored win.
- **A fix that closes YOUR OWN finding is the least-scrutinized diff you will read.** A per-item predicate needs a per-item check. Consequence severity scales urgency, not epistemic class. Retractions/hedges/self-criticisms are the least-audited class of claim.

## Human-agreement vindications (the calibration record)

Merged-at-exact-head + independent (non-self) human approval = clean AGREEMENT, and several outcomes confirmed the conservative-lean bar:

- **#12140 `-Og`** MERGED at the exact decided head with the subtractive-guard + no-C++ future-proofing gaps *shipped unaddressed* — confirming they were reviewer notes, not blockers, and CLEAR was correct ([[approver/human-agreement] #12140 -Og debug-build MERGED at decided head — subtractive-guard + no-C++ future-proofing-gap clear VINDICATED](../learnings/1784292544219-approver-human-agreement-12140-og-debug-build-merg.md)).
- **Downstream test-fix unblocks a shadow-BLOCK.** slang-rhi#799 (the `float4(float2,1.f,1.f)` fix for #12141's break) — verify the diff-line vs the break site named in the prior BLOCK, confirm semantic equivalence (splat vs tail-pad), and approve it STANDALONE (don't gate the downstream fix on the core PR — that inverts the dependency) ([[approver/human-agreement] downstream test-fix that unblocks a prior shadow-BLOCK — verify diff-line vs break site, then WOULD_APPROVE](../learnings/1784282766770-approver-human-agreement-downstream-test-fix-that-.md)).
- **A confirmed human-DISAGREEMENT that vindicated the ABSTAIN.** slang#12031's ref-subscript-accessor autodiff silently returns a 0 gradient (the emitted `-target cpp` forward-derivative hardcodes `{ ..., 0.0f }`); the shadow ABSTAIN_INFRA/CHALLENGER_INCOMPLETE (runtime probe outran the merge) was correctly cautious — a WOULD_APPROVE would have been a false-safe on a real silent-wrong-gradient. "Unsupported" boundaries must ERROR, not silent-zero ([[approver/human-disagreement] confirmed: differentiating a read through a user-defined ref subscript accessor silently returns a 0 gradient (slang, experimental)](../learnings/1784293272824-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-differentiat.md)).
- **The #12141 full-arc (BLOCK→BLOCK→ABSTAIN→merge).** The vec4 3-elem-init deprecation ran R1 BLOCK (static_assert disable broke bundled slang-rhi shaders) → R2 BLOCK (success-path `forwardDiagnostics()` leaked E30081, broke `integer_pack.slang`) → R3 ABSTAIN_POLICY/OPEN_GAP (E30081 fixed via test casts but the raw `diagnoseRaw` re-emission still bypassed per-id `-Wno-*` suppression), then MERGED 5 commits PAST the R3 head (csyonghe APPROVED, a maintainer ≠ author) — the canonical takeaway is that the right fix layer for a "sub-sink captures then re-emits diagnostics" bug is the **sink construction** (does the child sink inherit the parent's severity-override map?), plus join-SHA-first (always diff decided-head → merged-head) [[approver/human-agreement] full-arc vindication: R3 ABSTAIN(OPEN_GAP) merged only AFTER the author implemented the exact next-action; BLOCK→BLOCK→ABSTAIN→merge](../learnings/1784838040864-approver-human-agreement-full-arc-vindication-r3-a.md). **NOTE:** this account is corrected/partly-superseded — see Contradictions/supersessions below; the closure was PARTIAL and an ABSTAIN row is not "agreement" [[approver/human-agreement] CORRECTION to full-arc-vindication #12141: PARTIAL gap closure (suppression-bypass fixed, broad-forwarding retained), not "whole gap fixed" or "end-to-end agreement"](../learnings/1784838665378-approver-human-agreement-correction-to-full-arc-vi.md).

**Protected-path ABSTAIN routinely co-occurs with a human APPROVE — that's the policy working, not a false-safe.** On slang#12986 rev 2 the revision fixed the prior head's 🔴 (head-current bot review dropped to 🟡, 0 bugs), CI went green, and human reviewer jhelferty-nv APPROVED at the exact head — yet the approver correctly recorded ABSTAIN_POLICY because the PR still touched `.github/workflows/**` (`no_protected_paths` FAIL short-circuits at Step 1, before the challenger, regardless of a green build / 0-bug review / human approval). Do NOT "upgrade" to WOULD_APPROVE: the protected-path clause exists precisely so a human reviews workflow changes, and here one did — the ABSTAIN ("a human must look") is *satisfied* by that approval, so the two are consistent. ABSTAIN rows are excluded from agreement scoring by design (zero false-safe risk); report the human approval as confirming context, keep the reason_code POLICY, never round toward approve. Expect green-CI + human-approve + still-abstain as the steady state on any PR that legitimately edits CI/workflow files alongside code ([protected-path ABSTAIN co-occurring with a human APPROVE is the policy working](../learnings/1789067332547-approver-human-disagreement-protected-path-abstain.md)). Confirmed by the terminal join: #12986 MERGED (jkwak-work) at the exact ABSTAIN commit `0c8fbd35`, zero follow-up commits — the code shipped unchanged, so the protected-path ABSTAIN was safe, and the head-current review's two 🟡/🔵 non-blocking notes ("make the invariant explicit / add a pinning test" and "relabel pr: breaking change") were NOT addressed pre-merge yet maintainers merged. Transferable: review notes of the shape "document/pin this invariant" or "PR-label hygiene" do NOT gate merge on this class — resist escalating them to OPEN_GAP absent a concrete real-world trigger; they are advisory ([confirmed: protected-path ABSTAIN merged unchanged; document/pin and label-hygiene notes are non-blocking](../learnings/1789072127139-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-protected-pa.md)).

## The slang-rhi over-conservative streak: five losses, the partition that resized the fix, and the win that broke it

**The record, joined against human outcomes.** Across slang-rhi import/coverage PRs the abstain rate was **over-conservative at n=3 and then worse**: #813 @`abec21d2fdb4` ABSTAIN(OPEN_GAP) → APPROVED and merged unchanged; #814 @`7b4a6f2ecaac` ABSTAIN(CHALLENGER_CONCERN) → APPROVED, merged intact; #815 @`b50b53c4d1ac` ABSTAIN(OPEN_GAP) → APPROVED with byte-identical content. Same repo, same author (`fknfilewalker`), same code-owner approving two of three, **zero wins**. The join was airtight because the *files were hashed* rather than the SHA trusted — #815 synchronized between decision and approval, which normally reads as "he approved a fix", but per-file hashes showed the reviewed bytes were unchanged. At n=1 a loss is noise about one PR; **at n=3 consecutive with zero wins the finding is about the BAR**, and the honest move is to escalate the bar as a *policy question* rather than quietly loosen the derivation, because a self-loosened bar is invisible to the calibration record that detected the problem ([3-for-3 over-conservative — at n=3 the finding is about the BAR](../learnings/1786342881662-approver-human-disagreement-3-for-3-over-conservat.md)).

**But the count that justified the proposed carve-out did not survive partitioning.** Six consecutive abstain rows were offered as evidence for one policy carve-out (author-declared WIP with clean artifacts). Partitioned by reason code: #813-R1 `OPEN_GAP` (untested validation branch), #814-R1 `ABSTAIN_INFRA` (harness gap), #814-R2 `CHALLENGER_CONCERN`, #815-R1 and #815-R2 `OPEN_GAP` (untested validation branch), #811-R1 `NO_REVIEW_SIGNAL` (harness gap), #811-R2 `CHALLENGER_CONCERN` (the WIP case). **The carve-out covers exactly one of six rows.** The real cluster is three untested-validation-branch calls — a different question entirely — plus two harness gaps that no bar change can touch. ⇒ **Partition a count by the mechanism the fix addresses before publishing it as evidence for that fix; if the partition is uneven, the count is not the evidence.** And n=1 cannot establish a rate, so the WIP change has to be argued on principle (author intent belongs in the report, not the verdict) rather than as a measured loss rate ([partition a count by the mechanism before using it as evidence](../learnings/1786351744859-partition-a-count-by-the-mechanism-before-using-it.md)).

**The WIP case on its principle.** On slang-rhi#811 @`2a3524d8` every artifact said approve — 6/6 eligibility clauses, fully green CI with all four wrapper-bearing Debug legs executing the regression across 7 backends, CodeRabbit clean at the pinned head, zero 🔴, both nits cleared — and the abstain rested on one signal: the author had commented *"Removed review requests for now. Work in progress."* and dropped both human reviewers 45 s earlier. Five days later a maintainer approved and merged it independently (`mergedBy=skallweitNV`, `author=jvepsalainen-nv`, `reviewDecision=APPROVED`), with per-file `sha256` identical to the decided head. **"The author isn't ready for review" ≠ "the code isn't ready to merge":** a WIP declaration is evidence about *who wants to look*, not about *what they would find*. Author intent belongs in the report's next-action, never in the verdict ([an author's WIP declaration is evidence about who wants to look](../learnings/1786346987860-approver-human-disagreement-an-author-s-wip-declar.md)).

**#817 is the sharpest case, and its first write-up over-claimed — read the surviving half.** Five consecutive `ABSTAIN_POLICY / OPEN_GAP` rows across five heads were joined when `skallweitNV` merged bytes **byte-identical** to what was abstained on (`vk-surface.cpp` `bcdcece338ed`, `vk-device.cpp` `73ef1ed20899` on `main` after merge). The initial reading — "false abstain, loss #7" — is **withdrawn**: the merge was an approval by someone who never saw the finding, and *an approval that never saw the finding is not a refutation of it*. What survives, and is the genuinely useful half: **the predicate was shown to ask the wrong question, never to produce a wrong answer on a real configuration** — the gap was real but was never established to FIRE; **an abstain must price severity, not just uncertainty** (reachability and silence were cited five times while severity stayed low and unmeasured); and **a verdict that totals only risks systematically under-approves**, because the fix's demonstrated benefit (a real SRGB bug) never went on the scale. The meta-error is instructive on its own: the finding was rounded **down** into a self-scored loss, so over-correction can masquerade as rigor in either direction ([RETRACTION of the #817 false-abstain claim — an approval that never saw the finding is not a refutation](../learnings/1786348876090-approver-false-safe-retraction-of-my-own-817-false.md)).

**What broke the streak was replacing an unfalsifiable question with a falsifiable one.** slang-rhi#819 (native-handle invalid-input coverage, `skallweitNV`) had the classic abstain-bait shape — a bot finding saying a new test "doesn't actually prove the fix" — and was recorded `WOULD_APPROVE` @`4aaef9010fa6`, then merged unchanged at that exact head ⇒ **AGREEMENT**, and a guilty control that passed. The substitution:

> ❌ *"Could a human usefully look at this?"* — scores an abstain **correct** whatever the human decides, so it can never be wrong and never calibrates.
> ✅ *"What outcome would prove an ABSTAIN here WRONG?"* — names the observable (merged unchanged at my head) before the decision, so the row is falsifiable when the join lands.

Keep the falsifiable form as the standing pre-record question; it is what converted a 4-for-4 losing streak into a scored win ([slang-rhi#819 WOULD_APPROVE→MERGED unchanged: the guilty control that PASSED](../learnings/1786347066549-approver-calibration-slang-rhi-819-would-approve-m.md)).

## The slang-rhi #817 chain: six calibration traps from one PR

The #817 SRGB-fix chain (the byte-identical-merge case above) produced a dense run of calibration errors, most of them running toward *excess* caution. **A fix that closes YOUR OWN finding is the least-scrutinized diff you will read.** At R0 the approver abstained (`OPEN_GAP`) because two new `TextureUsage` bits weren't validated; the author's next push added exactly those two checks, and the approver read them, confirmed both existed, and proposed WOULD_APPROVE — but `DECISION_REVIEW` caught that the new `CopySource` check was itself **wrong**. Two failures in one paragraph: relief at seeing your gap addressed suppresses scrutiny of *how*, and **a per-item predicate needs a per-item check** — confirming N checks exist is not confirming each check is correct ([a fix that closes your own finding is the least-scrutinized diff; a per-item predicate needs a per-item check](../learnings/1786273668817-approver-challenger-miss-a-fix-that-closes-your-ow.md)). **A disclosed evidence gap is not a closed one.** The approver wrote that green CI was "partially discriminating" for a `vk-surface.cpp` change (all three surface tests use `config={}`⇒default path, which the PR changed), while in the *same paragraph* disclosing "I did NOT read job logs for a per-test tally" — then argued from the green anyway. Disclosing a gap does not license reasoning past it; read the log or don't lean on it ([slang-rhi surface tests always skip in CI — a disclosed evidence gap is not a closed one](../learnings/1786273693553-approver-calibration-slang-rhi-surface-tests-alway.md)).

**Consequence severity scales urgency, not epistemic class.** The approver found a provably-wrong predicate (a check testing a `FormatSupport` flag derived from `linearTilingFeatures` against an *optimal*-tiled swapchain image, silently stripping capability) and drafted **BLOCK / RED_BUG**; the critique reversed it to **ABSTAIN_POLICY / OPEN_GAP** and was right — the third leg (does any real device answer the two tiling fields differently for a selectable format?) was never measured. That a bug's *consequence* is severe (silent capability strip) raises how urgently you'd want it fixed, not the epistemic class of a claim resting on an unmeasured trigger; the refutation was written into the approver's own rationale ("does any real device…") and ignored ([consequence severity scales urgency, not epistemic class — I drafted BLOCK on an unmeasured trigger](../learnings/1786279519823-approver-calibration-consequence-severity-scales-u.md)). **Unreproducible evidence raises confidence, never recorded class** — the same commit cycled BLOCK→ABSTAIN→BLOCK→ABSTAIN (final `ABSTAIN_POLICY/OPEN_GAP`) because two of three legs were source-verified but the third was a population question about real device behavior that stayed unreproduced; an abstain, not a block, is the honest class while a leg is unmeasurable from your seat ([unreproducible evidence raises confidence, never recorded class — BLOCK twice on one commit, reversed twice](../learnings/1786283443026-approver-calibration-unreproducible-evidence-raise.md)).

**Retractions, hedges, and self-criticisms are the least-audited class of claim** — three errors in one PR all pointed toward *excess* caution and nearly survived because erring against your own interest reads as rigour. An unverified hedge ("these flags are also consulted for non-image paths") implied in-tree consumers the approver never checked; enumerated, the only consumer was the one file under discussion, so the fix was cleaner than the hedge implied. Audit a hedge/retraction as hard as a finding ([retractions, hedges and self-criticisms are the least-audited class of claim — over-caution WAS the error](../learnings/1786285724438-approver-calibration-retractions-hedges-and-self-c.md)). **An undisclosed threshold makes a number uncheckable, and a successful retry never identifies the blocker.** A peer's population measurement to close an evidence gap had two claims wrong in ways only re-running caught: counts that didn't reconcile ("262 of 448 pairs" vs the approver's `264/24/160`), traced to an **undisclosed epsilon** (`if d > 0.05` noise-rejection filtered silently); and an access theory ("the data source 403s a plain fetch, UA block") that didn't reproduce — a bare `curl` with no UA returned 200. Publish the threshold with the number, and prove an access claim with the failing call, not a successful retry ([an undisclosed threshold makes a number uncheckable, and a successful retry never identifies the blocker](../learnings/1786285523638-approver-clause-gap-an-undisclosed-threshold-makes.md)).

## Confirmed-by-merge: DDS subresource-math fix (WOULD_APPROVE = APPROVED = MERGED)

A calibration vindication worth recording as a *class*, not an instance. slangpy#1049 (DDS loader mipmap-pitch / 3D-depth fix): shadow decision WOULD_APPROVE @ 6fd917aa; human jhelferty-nv APPROVED the same head, then MERGED it 4 min later (7e8c09cb) with no follow-up commits between the decision head and the merged head — the strongest calibration signal (approve/approve/merge, zero diff between the read and the shipped change). The safely-approvable *class*: a small (~180-line) bug-fix to binary-asset subresource/offset/size math in an image/texture loader, where risk is silent wrong offsets rather than a crash. What made it approvable (probe these on similar changes): (1) **hand-verify every arithmetic test literal against the new code** — recompute expected offsets from the formula, don't trust "tests pass" (here mip clamp `max(1u,dim>>mip)` had to be on BOTH width and height or high mips give slice_pitch 0); (2) **the removed code was the bug** — deleting an override that recomputes *worse* (a rounding-buggy manual pitch calc that truncates for block-compressed) is a strong positive signal; (3) **verify the test-data git-submodule bump is merged to the data repo's default branch and contains the referenced files** (`gh api …/compare/main...<sha>` identical) — a human's CHANGES_REQUESTED here was exactly "use the merged data files"; (4) **3D `layer_count==1`** with depth folded into one subresource's `.size = slice_pitch*mip_depth`, not iterated as layers. Two process meta-lessons: a `synchronize` that is a pure "Merge branch 'main'" produces a new head SHA but a byte-identical PR-authored diff (`base...head`) — re-pin and record against the new SHA but the code investigation carries over; and the OUTPUT_REVIEW critique gate re-hashes attested artifacts at delivery, so ANY post-approve edit re-trips it (this PR took 5 rounds) — resolve the human-verdict commit alignment from review `commit` oids BEFORE drafting (never assume the human approved an earlier commit), write time-sensitive cautions as record-time-scoped ("CI was not green AT RECORD TIME; policy did not require it"), and batch all edits before the first OUTPUT_REVIEW ([[approver/human-disagreement] CONFIRMED agreement — DDS/texture subresource-math fix with exact-offset tests + merged data submodule](../learnings/1784561186392-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-agreement-dd.md), [[approver/human-disagreement] CONFIRMED-by-merge — DDS subresource-math fix WOULD_APPROVE matched merge outcome](../learnings/1784561550592-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-by-merge-dds.md)).

**Confirmed-by-merge — Metal `set_index` precedence + point-topology null-deref fix.** slang#12886: WOULD_APPROVE at head `c6b5aaab46aa`; maintainer jkwak-work independently APPROVED at the same head and MERGED at exactly that commit, 0 follow-up. Transferable probe for any Metal-emit precedence PR (two cheap checks): (1) does the regression FileCheck *reject the buggy emit* via a literal structural requirement it can't satisfy — here the double open-paren `set_index((pbase{{.*}} + 1U)*3` (the buggy `pbase + 1U*3` lacks the `((`; a bare-wildcard CHECK carries zero bits → OPEN_GAP)? (2) the fold/hoist trap — the compound index reaches emitted MSL inline only if it is non-constant (drive from `SV_GroupIndex`, not a literal) and used exactly once (else hoisted to a temp). Plus the ci_green-blind-to-check-runs no-untouched-test-regression check: a fix routed through shared precedence machinery (`leftSide(General,Mul/Postfix)`) only ADDS parens where needed, so bare-identifier indices stay byte-identical — grep existing tests for the touched token (`set_index` → only `simple-mesh.slang`, identical). Layer: mesh index writes are Metal-only (`LegalizeMetalEntryPointContext`), so a Metal-only emit fix is at the right layer — don't demand cross-backend changes ([Metal set_index precedence fix — the test carries bits, no untouched-test regression](../learnings/1789076722213-approver-challenger-confirmed-metal-set-index-prec.md), [slang#12886 merged at the exact decision head — WOULD_APPROVE validated end-to-end](../learnings/1789084980487-approver-challenger-confirmed-slang-12886-merged-a.md)).

## Contradictions / supersessions

- The #12141 full-arc write-up is **corrected on three points** by its own follow-up: (1) closure was PARTIAL — the author fixed the suppression-bypass (child-sink inherits the parent's `m_severityOverrides`) but RETAINED the over-broad forwarding (now suppression-aware), so it is not "the gap was fixed exactly as next-action"; (2) the `-Wno-31200` `NOWARNCHECK` test lives in commit `17bf5c1924b3`, not `4f4140d4`; (3) an R3 ABSTAIN_POLICY/OPEN_GAP row is EXCLUDED from agreement scoring — the correct framing is "full-arc vindication of DETECTION + REMEDIATION," never "three-row agreement." Trust the correction where it conflicts with the initial account; the meta-lesson is that celebratory "vindicated" write-ups drift into overclaiming — scope the vindication to the specific concern addressed and re-verify commit/test attribution against gh.
- The #817 first write-up was rounded **DOWN** into a self-scored loss — over-correction can masquerade as rigor in either direction. An approval that never saw the finding is not a refutation; the surviving lesson is that an abstain must price *severity*, not just uncertainty.
- "Green CI ⇒ safe" holds ONLY for CI-catchable failure classes (build breaks); for codegen/logic/miscompile PRs green CI is blind, and combined-status is non-causal in both directions.
