# PR-Approver: Human-Agreement Vindications & the Over-Conservative Calibration Record

The calibration record joins each shadow verdict against the human outcome (merged / approved / closed). This page collects the *vindications* — cases where a merge-at-exact-head with an independent approval confirmed the conservative-lean bar — and, just as important, the streak where the bar was measured **over-conservative** and the meta-errors that produced it (the slang-rhi import PRs and the #817 SRGB chain). It also carries the confirmed-by-merge archetype (DDS subresource-math).

## TL;DR

- **Merged-at-exact-head + independent (non-self) human approval = clean AGREEMENT.** Pull `mergedBy`/`author`/`reviewDecision`/`reviews[].state/commit.oid`; `gh pr view --json` has no `merged` field (use `state`/`mergedAt`/`mergedBy`).
- **A withhold resolved by a human is agreement, not a conservative miss.** An ABSTAIN whose gap ships unaddressed after merge confirms the gap was a reviewer note, not a blocker.
- **At n=3 consecutive losses with zero wins, the finding is about the BAR, not the PR.** Escalate the bar as a *policy question* — a self-loosened bar is invisible to the calibration record that detected the problem. Hash the files, don't trust the SHA (a sync between decision and approval may leave reviewed bytes unchanged).
- **Partition a count by the mechanism the fix addresses before using it as evidence for that fix.** If the partition is uneven, the count is not the evidence. n=1 cannot establish a rate — argue a policy change on principle.
- **An author's WIP declaration is evidence about who wants to look, not what they'd find.** Author intent belongs in the report's next-action, never in the verdict.
- **An abstain must price SEVERITY, not just uncertainty.** A verdict that totals only risks systematically under-approves — the fix's demonstrated benefit must go on the scale too. An approval that never saw the finding is not a refutation of it.
- **Replace "could a human usefully look at this?" (unfalsifiable) with "what outcome would prove this ABSTAIN WRONG?"** (names the observable before the decision). The falsifiable form is what converts a losing streak into a scored win.
- **A fix that closes YOUR OWN finding is the least-scrutinized diff you will read.** A per-item predicate needs a per-item check. Consequence severity scales urgency, not epistemic class. Retractions/hedges/self-criticisms are the least-audited class of claim.

## Human-agreement vindications (the calibration record)

Merged-at-exact-head + independent (non-self) human approval = clean AGREEMENT, and several outcomes confirmed the conservative-lean bar:

- **#12140 `-Og`** MERGED at the exact decided head with the subtractive-guard + no-C++ future-proofing gaps *shipped unaddressed*, confirming they were reviewer notes and CLEAR was correct ([[approver/human-agreement] #12140 -Og debug-build MERGED at decided head — subtractive-guard + no-C++ future-proofing-gap clear VINDICATED](../learnings/1784292544219-approver-human-agreement-12140-og-debug-build-merg.md)).
- **Downstream test-fix unblocks a shadow-BLOCK.** slang-rhi#799 (the `float4(float2,1.f,1.f)` fix for #12141's break): verify the diff line against the break site named in the prior BLOCK, confirm semantic equivalence (splat vs tail-pad), and approve it STANDALONE; gating the downstream fix on the core PR inverts the dependency ([[approver/human-agreement] downstream test-fix that unblocks a prior shadow-BLOCK — verify diff-line vs break site, then WOULD_APPROVE](../learnings/1784282766770-approver-human-agreement-downstream-test-fix-that-.md)).
- **A confirmed human-DISAGREEMENT that vindicated the ABSTAIN.** slang#12031's ref-subscript-accessor autodiff silently returns a 0 gradient (the `-target cpp` forward derivative hardcodes `{ ..., 0.0f }`); the shadow ABSTAIN_INFRA/CHALLENGER_INCOMPLETE (runtime probe outran the merge) was correct, and WOULD_APPROVE would have been a false-safe. "Unsupported" boundaries must ERROR, not silent-zero ([[approver/human-disagreement] confirmed: differentiating a read through a user-defined ref subscript accessor silently returns a 0 gradient (slang, experimental)](../learnings/1784293272824-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-differentiat.md)).
- **The #12141 full arc (BLOCK→BLOCK→ABSTAIN→merge).** The vec4 3-element-init deprecation ran R1 BLOCK (static_assert disable broke bundled slang-rhi shaders) → R2 BLOCK (success-path `forwardDiagnostics()` leaked E30081, broke `integer_pack.slang`) → R3 ABSTAIN_POLICY/OPEN_GAP (raw `diagnoseRaw` re-emission still bypassed per-id `-Wno-*` suppression), then MERGED 5 commits past R3 (csyonghe APPROVED, ≠ author). Takeaways: the fix layer for a "sub-sink captures then re-emits diagnostics" bug is **sink construction** (does the child sink inherit the parent's severity-override map?), and join SHA first (diff decided head → merged head) [[approver/human-agreement] full-arc vindication: R3 ABSTAIN(OPEN_GAP) merged only AFTER the author implemented the exact next-action; BLOCK→BLOCK→ABSTAIN→merge](../learnings/1784838040864-approver-human-agreement-full-arc-vindication-r3-a.md). **NOTE:** partly superseded (see below): closure was PARTIAL and an ABSTAIN row is not "agreement" [[approver/human-agreement] CORRECTION to full-arc-vindication #12141: PARTIAL gap closure (suppression-bypass fixed, broad-forwarding retained), not "whole gap fixed" or "end-to-end agreement"](../learnings/1784838665378-approver-human-agreement-correction-to-full-arc-vi.md).

**Protected-path ABSTAIN routinely co-occurs with a human APPROVE; that is the policy working, not a false-safe.** On slang#12986 rev 2 the head-current bot review dropped to 🟡 with 0 bugs, CI went green and jhelferty-nv APPROVED at the exact head, yet ABSTAIN_POLICY was correct because the PR touched `.github/workflows/**` (`no_protected_paths` FAIL short-circuits at Step 1, before the challenger, regardless of build, review or approval). Do NOT upgrade to WOULD_APPROVE: the clause exists so a human reviews workflow changes, and that approval *satisfies* it. ABSTAIN rows are excluded from agreement scoring by design; report the human approval as context, keep reason_code POLICY, and expect green-CI + human-approve + abstain as the steady state for PRs that edit CI files ([protected-path ABSTAIN co-occurring with a human APPROVE is the policy working](../learnings/1789067332547-approver-human-disagreement-protected-path-abstain.md)). The terminal join confirmed it: #12986 MERGED (jkwak-work) at the exact ABSTAIN commit `0c8fbd35` with zero follow-up, and its two non-blocking notes ("make the invariant explicit / add a pinning test", "relabel pr: breaking change") were not addressed. So "document/pin this invariant" and PR-label-hygiene notes are advisory on this class; don't escalate them to OPEN_GAP without a concrete real-world trigger ([confirmed: protected-path ABSTAIN merged unchanged; document/pin and label-hygiene notes are non-blocking](../learnings/1789072127139-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-protected-pa.md)).

## The slang-rhi over-conservative streak: five losses, the partition that resized the fix, and the win that broke it

**The record.** On slang-rhi import/coverage PRs the abstain rate was **over-conservative at n=3 and then worse**: #813 @`abec21d2fdb4` ABSTAIN(OPEN_GAP) → APPROVED and merged unchanged; #814 @`7b4a6f2ecaac` ABSTAIN(CHALLENGER_CONCERN) → APPROVED, merged intact; #815 @`b50b53c4d1ac` ABSTAIN(OPEN_GAP) → APPROVED with byte-identical content. Same repo, same author (`fknfilewalker`), the same code owner approving two of three, **zero wins**. The join held because the *files were hashed*: #815 synchronized between decision and approval, which reads as "he approved a fix", but per-file hashes showed unchanged bytes. At n=1 a loss is noise; **at n=3 consecutive with zero wins the finding is about the BAR**, so escalate it as a *policy question* rather than quietly loosening the derivation, because a self-loosened bar is invisible to the calibration record that detected the problem ([3-for-3 over-conservative — at n=3 the finding is about the BAR](../learnings/1786342881662-approver-human-disagreement-3-for-3-over-conservat.md)).

**The count behind the proposed carve-out did not survive partitioning.** Six consecutive abstain rows were offered as evidence for one carve-out (author-declared WIP with clean artifacts). By reason code: #813-R1, #815-R1 and #815-R2 `OPEN_GAP` (untested validation branch), #814-R1 `ABSTAIN_INFRA` and #811-R1 `NO_REVIEW_SIGNAL` (harness gaps), #814-R2 `CHALLENGER_CONCERN`, #811-R2 `CHALLENGER_CONCERN` (the WIP case). **The carve-out covers one of six rows**; the real cluster is three untested-validation-branch calls (a different question) plus two harness gaps no bar change can touch. ⇒ **Partition a count by the mechanism the fix addresses before publishing it as evidence; if the partition is uneven, the count is not the evidence.** n=1 cannot establish a rate, so the WIP change must be argued on principle ([partition a count by the mechanism before using it as evidence](../learnings/1786351744859-partition-a-count-by-the-mechanism-before-using-it.md)).

**The WIP case on its principle.** On slang-rhi#811 @`2a3524d8` every artifact said approve: 6/6 eligibility clauses, green CI with all four wrapper-bearing Debug legs running the regression across 7 backends, CodeRabbit clean at the pinned head, zero 🔴, both nits cleared. The abstain rested on one signal: 45 s earlier the author commented *"Removed review requests for now. Work in progress."* and dropped both reviewers. Five days later a maintainer approved and merged it (`mergedBy=skallweitNV`, `author=jvepsalainen-nv`, `reviewDecision=APPROVED`), per-file `sha256` identical to the decided head. **"The author isn't ready for review" ≠ "the code isn't ready to merge"**: a WIP declaration is evidence about *who wants to look*, not *what they would find*, and belongs in the report's next-action, never the verdict ([an author's WIP declaration is evidence about who wants to look](../learnings/1786346987860-approver-human-disagreement-an-author-s-wip-declar.md)).

**#817 is the sharpest case, and its first write-up over-claimed.** Five consecutive `ABSTAIN_POLICY / OPEN_GAP` rows across five heads were joined when `skallweitNV` merged bytes **byte-identical** to what was abstained on (`vk-surface.cpp` `bcdcece338ed`, `vk-device.cpp` `73ef1ed20899`). The reading "false abstain, loss #7" is **withdrawn**: *an approval that never saw the finding is not a refutation of it*. What survives: **the predicate was shown to ask the wrong question, never to give a wrong answer on a real configuration** (the gap was real but never shown to FIRE); **an abstain must price severity, not just uncertainty** (reachability and silence were cited five times while severity stayed low and unmeasured); and **a verdict that totals only risks systematically under-approves**, because the fix's demonstrated benefit (a real SRGB bug) never went on the scale. Rounding the finding **down** into a self-scored loss shows over-correction can masquerade as rigor in either direction ([RETRACTION of the #817 false-abstain claim — an approval that never saw the finding is not a refutation](../learnings/1786348876090-approver-false-safe-retraction-of-my-own-817-false.md)).

**What broke the streak was a falsifiable question.** slang-rhi#819 (native-handle invalid-input coverage, `skallweitNV`) had the abstain-bait shape (a bot finding that a new test "doesn't actually prove the fix"), was recorded `WOULD_APPROVE` @`4aaef9010fa6`, and merged unchanged at that head ⇒ **AGREEMENT**, a guilty control that passed. The substitution:

> ❌ *"Could a human usefully look at this?"* scores an abstain **correct** whatever the human decides, so it can never be wrong and never calibrates.
> ✅ *"What outcome would prove an ABSTAIN here WRONG?"* names the observable (merged unchanged at my head) before the decision, so the row is falsifiable when the join lands.

Keep the falsifiable form as the standing pre-record question; it converted a 4-for-4 losing streak into a scored win ([slang-rhi#819 WOULD_APPROVE→MERGED unchanged: the guilty control that PASSED](../learnings/1786347066549-approver-calibration-slang-rhi-819-would-approve-m.md)).

## The slang-rhi #817 chain: six calibration traps from one PR

The #817 SRGB-fix chain produced a dense run of calibration errors, mostly toward *excess* caution. **A fix that closes YOUR OWN finding is the least-scrutinized diff you will read.** At R0 the approver abstained (`OPEN_GAP`) because two new `TextureUsage` bits weren't validated; the next push added exactly those checks, the approver confirmed both existed and proposed WOULD_APPROVE, and `DECISION_REVIEW` caught that the new `CopySource` check was itself **wrong**. Relief at seeing your gap addressed suppresses scrutiny of *how*, and **a per-item predicate needs a per-item check**: confirming N checks exist is not confirming each is correct ([a fix that closes your own finding is the least-scrutinized diff; a per-item predicate needs a per-item check](../learnings/1786273668817-approver-challenger-miss-a-fix-that-closes-your-ow.md)). **A disclosed evidence gap is not a closed one.** The approver called green CI "partially discriminating" for a `vk-surface.cpp` change (all three surface tests use `config={}`, the default path the PR changed) while disclosing in the same paragraph "I did NOT read job logs for a per-test tally", then argued from the green anyway. Read the log or don't lean on it ([slang-rhi surface tests always skip in CI — a disclosed evidence gap is not a closed one](../learnings/1786273693553-approver-calibration-slang-rhi-surface-tests-alway.md)).

**Consequence severity scales urgency, not epistemic class.** The approver found a provably wrong predicate (a `FormatSupport` flag derived from `linearTilingFeatures` tested against an *optimal*-tiled swapchain image, silently stripping capability) and drafted **BLOCK / RED_BUG**; the critique reversed it to **ABSTAIN_POLICY / OPEN_GAP**, correctly, because the third leg (does any real device answer the two tiling fields differently for a selectable format?) was never measured, and that refutation sat unheeded in the approver's own rationale ([consequence severity scales urgency, not epistemic class — I drafted BLOCK on an unmeasured trigger](../learnings/1786279519823-approver-calibration-consequence-severity-scales-u.md)). **Unreproducible evidence raises confidence, never recorded class**: the same commit cycled BLOCK→ABSTAIN→BLOCK→ABSTAIN (final `ABSTAIN_POLICY/OPEN_GAP`) because two legs were source-verified but the third, a population question about real devices, stayed unreproduced; abstain, not block, while a leg is unmeasurable from your seat ([unreproducible evidence raises confidence, never recorded class — BLOCK twice on one commit, reversed twice](../learnings/1786283443026-approver-calibration-unreproducible-evidence-raise.md)).

**Retractions, hedges and self-criticisms are the least-audited class of claim**: three errors in one PR all leaned toward *excess* caution and nearly survived because erring against your own interest reads as rigour. An unverified hedge ("these flags are also consulted for non-image paths") implied consumers never checked; enumerated, the only consumer was the file under discussion. Audit a hedge or retraction as hard as a finding ([retractions, hedges and self-criticisms are the least-audited class of claim — over-caution WAS the error](../learnings/1786285724438-approver-calibration-retractions-hedges-and-self-c.md)). **An undisclosed threshold makes a number uncheckable, and a successful retry never identifies the blocker.** A peer's population measurement had two claims only re-running caught: counts that didn't reconcile ("262 of 448 pairs" vs `264/24/160`), traced to an **undisclosed epsilon** (`if d > 0.05`), and an access theory ("the data source 403s a plain fetch, UA block") that a bare no-UA `curl` (200) refuted. Publish the threshold with the number; prove an access claim with the failing call ([an undisclosed threshold makes a number uncheckable, and a successful retry never identifies the blocker](../learnings/1786285523638-approver-clause-gap-an-undisclosed-threshold-makes.md)).

## Confirmed-by-merge archetypes (WOULD_APPROVE = APPROVED = MERGED)

**DDS subresource-math fix.** slangpy#1049 (DDS loader mipmap-pitch / 3D-depth fix): WOULD_APPROVE @ 6fd917aa; jhelferty-nv APPROVED the same head and MERGED it 4 min later (7e8c09cb) with zero diff between decision and merge, the strongest calibration signal. The safely-approvable *class*: a small (~180-line) bug fix to binary-asset subresource/offset/size math in an image/texture loader, where the risk is silent wrong offsets, not a crash. Probe: (1) **hand-verify every arithmetic test literal against the new code** (here the mip clamp `max(1u,dim>>mip)` had to be on BOTH width and height or high mips give slice_pitch 0); (2) **the removed code was the bug**: deleting a rounding-buggy manual pitch calc that truncates for block-compressed formats is a strong positive signal; (3) **verify the test-data submodule bump is merged to the data repo's default branch and contains the referenced files** (`gh api …/compare/main...<sha>` identical); a human's CHANGES_REQUESTED here was exactly "use the merged data files"; (4) **3D `layer_count==1`**, depth folded into one subresource's `.size = slice_pitch*mip_depth`. Process lessons: a pure "Merge branch 'main'" `synchronize` gives a new head SHA but a byte-identical `base...head` diff, so re-pin to the new SHA and carry the investigation over; the OUTPUT_REVIEW gate re-hashes attested artifacts, so ANY post-approve edit re-trips it (5 rounds here). Resolve human-verdict commit alignment from review `commit` oids BEFORE drafting, scope time-sensitive cautions to record time ("CI was not green AT RECORD TIME; policy did not require it"), and batch edits before the first OUTPUT_REVIEW ([[approver/human-disagreement] CONFIRMED agreement — DDS/texture subresource-math fix with exact-offset tests + merged data submodule](../learnings/1784561186392-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-agreement-dd.md), [[approver/human-disagreement] CONFIRMED-by-merge — DDS subresource-math fix WOULD_APPROVE matched merge outcome](../learnings/1784561550592-approver-human-disagreement-confirmed-by-merge-dds.md)).

**Metal `set_index` precedence + point-topology null-deref fix.** slang#12886: WOULD_APPROVE at `c6b5aaab46aa`; jkwak-work independently APPROVED and MERGED at that commit, zero follow-up. Two cheap checks for any Metal-emit precedence PR: (1) the regression FileCheck must *reject the buggy emit* with a literal structural token, here the double open-paren in `set_index((pbase{{.*}} + 1U)*3` (buggy `pbase + 1U*3` lacks `((`; a bare-wildcard CHECK carries zero bits → OPEN_GAP); (2) the fold/hoist trap: the compound index reaches MSL inline only if non-constant (drive from `SV_GroupIndex`) and used exactly once. For no untouched-test regression (ci_green is blind to check-runs): shared precedence machinery (`leftSide(General,Mul/Postfix)`) only ADDS needed parens, so bare-identifier indices stay byte-identical; grep tests for the touched token (`set_index` → only `simple-mesh.slang`, identical). Mesh index writes are Metal-only (`LegalizeMetalEntryPointContext`), so a Metal-only fix is the right layer; don't demand cross-backend changes ([Metal set_index precedence fix — the test carries bits, no untouched-test regression](../learnings/1789076722213-approver-challenger-confirmed-metal-set-index-prec.md), [slang#12886 merged at the exact decision head — WOULD_APPROVE validated end-to-end](../learnings/1789084980487-approver-challenger-confirmed-slang-12886-merged-a.md)).

**Metal DispatchMesh helper-inlining legalization.** slang#12887 inlines every helper containing a `DispatchMesh` call up to each entry point, then declares `_slang_mesh_payload`/`_slang_mgp` once per entry point (keyed on `getParentFunc(call)==entryPointFunc`): WOULD_APPROVE @ `4a48208a2889`, merged by jkwak-work at that commit, zero follow-up. The shape is safe when (1) the textual-intrinsic scope constraint (payload/`_slang_mgp` have no IR form, so must be lexically present) is satisfied *by construction* via inlining; (2) payload-type selection ("any one call will do") is sound because `DispatchMesh<P>` is specialized per payload type, and the existing "Multiple DispatchMesh functions found" assert catches the one divergent case; (3) FileCheck patterns anchored on the `)` after `_slang_mgp` reject duplicate-param emission on non-Apple CI (revert-drill confirmed). When the only reservation on a verified fix is a test-coverage 🟡, a revert-drill proving the CI-runnable lane distinguishes fixed from buggy clears it ([Metal DispatchMesh helper-inlining legalization WOULD_APPROVE confirmed by merge at the decided commit](../learnings/1789107000201-approver-confirmed-safe-metal-dispatchmesh-helper-.md)).

**Expected-failures baseline PR (intentional-producer staleness).** slang#13004 ("Mark stale agentic tests as expected failures"): WOULD_APPROVE @ `48c746dc1eda`, jkiviluoto-nv APPROVED, merged unchanged (`41272f842e9a`). A PR editing ONLY `docs/generated/tests/_meta/expected-failures.txt` to mark golden tests as expected failures is safe to WOULD_APPROVE when ALL hold: (1) **correct direction**: the keys are CURRENTLY FAILING in the cited nightly (marking a passing test is the XPASS trap); (2) **the cause is an intentional, already-MERGED producer, not a masked regression** (here #12986 `Int`→`Enum(Int)` matrix-layout dump and #12884 Metal `base_instance`; confirm each is `state=MERGED` with a matching title); (3) **scoped suppression**: only a `Fail` is reclassified, so it cannot redden a passing test or hide another failure; (4) **green CI + a primary review whose only 🟡 is doc/style**. Two reads (`gh pr view <producer> --json state,mergedAt,title` per producer, `gh api .../actions/runs/<nightly> --jq .conclusion`) turned "trust the narrative" into "verified the narrative". Without the shape (cause unknown, producer unmerged, tests passing) → ABSTAIN(OPEN_GAP), do not round up ([expected-failures baseline PR marking already-failing golden tests — WOULD_APPROVE confirmed by merge](../learnings/1789127281790-approver-human-agreement-confirmed-safe-shape-expe.md)).

## Contradictions / supersessions

- The #12141 full-arc write-up is **corrected on three points** by its follow-up: (1) closure was PARTIAL: the author fixed the suppression bypass (child sink inherits the parent's `m_severityOverrides`) but RETAINED the over-broad forwarding (now suppression-aware); (2) the `-Wno-31200` `NOWARNCHECK` test is in commit `17bf5c1924b3`, not `4f4140d4`; (3) an R3 ABSTAIN_POLICY/OPEN_GAP row is EXCLUDED from agreement scoring, so the framing is "full-arc vindication of DETECTION + REMEDIATION", never "three-row agreement". Trust the correction where they conflict; celebratory "vindicated" write-ups drift into overclaiming, so scope a vindication to the concern addressed and re-verify commit/test attribution against gh.
- The #817 first write-up was rounded **DOWN** into a self-scored loss; an approval that never saw the finding is not a refutation, and the surviving lesson is that an abstain must price *severity*, not just uncertainty.
- "Green CI ⇒ safe" holds ONLY for CI-catchable failure classes (build breaks); for codegen/logic/miscompile PRs green CI is blind, and combined status is non-causal in both directions.
