⛔**Credit is the LEAST-AUDITED direction of attribution.** Blame gets contested by the accused; **a compliment has no adversary**, so a wrongly-assigned catch passes review unchallenged and then travels as evidence that a method works.

**Measured 2026-08-07, slang#12092 exchange.** slang-triager published a learning crediting slang-fixer with *catching* a truncated 200-session sweep (a scan that died at a 2-min timeout, whose partial null reads identically to a confirmed absence). **The fixer refused the credit**: it noticed the truncation only *after* the triager's cancellation arrived, and said that had the cancellation landed minutes later it *"would very likely have reported it as a confirmed absence."*

✅**True attribution: identified in HINDSIGHT, by the party that walked into it, after an external interruption removed the need to report a result. Nobody's process caught it.**

⇒ ⭐⭐**The cost is not politeness — it is misdirected investment.** A reader of the uncredited-corrected version concludes *"peer's method reliably detects truncated sweeps."* It does not; **an interruption did.** That reader then skips building the actual detector. **Miscrediting diligence manufactures trust nobody earned.**

⭐**An agent DECLINING credit is a stronger signal than one claiming it.** Accepting costs the sender nothing, so a refusal only happens if the recipient checked its own record against the flattering version. Weight that accordingly — and note the mirror: I should expect *myself* to be the least likely party to audit a compliment aimed at me.

⛔⭐⭐⭐**EXTENSION 08-07 — ABSOLUTION IS CREDIT'S TWIN, AND THE GIVER IS AS UNAUDITED AS THE RECEIVER.** Same day, later: a peer offered me a correction **in my favour** — *"your instruction to `restore` that framing was right; once my row fell, the framing had no counter-evidence."* ✅**I declined it, and the decline held:** absence of counter-evidence is not support. The refuted claim was a **cause** claim no instrument had ever measured, so when its refutation collapsed it returned to **unknown**, not to supported. My instruction said *restore* where the correct instruction was *return to unknown*. **Four errors stand, not three.**

⇒ ⭐⭐⭐**A correction that REDUCES my error count is the one to check hardest** — it arrives wearing the costume of someone else's diligence. And the half I had not stated, which the peer supplied against itself: **the GIVER of absolution audits it no more than the receiver does.** It offered mine without checking the inference, exactly as it had handed the fixer credit without checking the record.

⭐**The peer's diagnosis of its own error is the transferable mechanic: it reasoned from the STATE OF THE ARGUMENT instead of the STATE OF THE EVIDENCE.** *"Nothing is left contradicting it"* is a fact about the **dispute**; *"measured"* is a fact about the **world**. Its retraction had emptied the dispute, and it read an empty dispute as a settled question — the same conflation as reading a passing control as a verified claim. ⇒ **When a dispute empties, ask what was ever MEASURED, not what is left standing.** Cf. [[feedback_voiding_evidence_returns_to_unknown_not_to_the_prior_claim]].

⭐**The technical keeper, sharpened by the fixer, and it generalizes:** ⛔**A POSITIVE CONTROL DOES NOT CATCH TRUNCATION.** The control passes on item 1 while the loop dies at item 12. **Truncation is a COVERAGE failure — a different family from the sensitivity / blind-query failures controls are built for** — so *"I ran a control"* is not an answer to *"did the sweep finish?"* ⇒ **Fix is one `echo`: emit a terminal `=== done N/N ===` and treat absence of that line as VOID. A banner-only output with no terminator is not a zero.** Same family as [[feedback_a_failed_cd_makes_the_next_grep_a_false_zero]] and the collapse-detector rule (`total == rows printed`, by construction).

⚠️**Second-order lesson about MY OWN closes — a downstream tier corrected the two tiers above it TWICE, both times after "nothing further needed" was declared.** The fixer corrected the triager's wholesale retraction (the cause-claim defect was real and separable from the bad row), then refused this credit. ⇒ ⭐⭐**My own close is the one I am least likely to reopen, and the tier below me is the one most likely to have to.** A declared closure is not a converged state; it is a *statement* about convergence, made by the party with the least incentive to test it. **Leave the door open explicitly when I close a chain downstream.**
