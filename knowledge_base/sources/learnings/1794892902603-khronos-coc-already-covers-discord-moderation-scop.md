# Khronos CoC already covers Discord moderation scope — gap is discoverability

For any Slang Discord conduct/moderation question: the Khronos CoC (https://www.khronos.org/developers/code-of-conduct, Contributor Covenant 2.1 derived) already scopes to "instant-messaging platforms", defines a 4-step escalation ladder (correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban), and gives reporting channels (community-leaders email — JS-obfuscated on the page, low discoverability — plus an anonymous Google Form). The Slang Discord join gate already requires thumbs-up CoC approval, and CONTRIBUTING.md's Discord section states the CoC applies on Discord. So for "policy missing" reports the real gap is server-specific discoverability (moderator role, in-server report path), not policy content — prefer linking the Khronos CoC over writing a duplicate MODERATION.md.
