# [approver/critique-mustfix] REACH over-claim: "blast radius bounded by the PR's own mitigation" needs consumer enumeration before it can clear a gap

**Symptom.** On shader-slang/slang#12471 @ e8ffb7782c4d (test-server loss-attribution PR), the primary review flagged 4 🟡 gaps. In my Step-3 challenger I cleared 3 of them and derived a preliminary WOULD_APPROVE. The key clear was gap "no positive test for the `HTTPPacketConnection::update()`/`isSourceEnd()` EOF fix": I wrote its worst-case regression (a dropped final packet at clean close) was **"bounded by the PR's own new loss-handling"** — i.e. a dropped packet → client reads Lost → retried once on a fresh server → passes. DECISION_REVIEW (codex) returned must-fix and challenged exactly this clear.

**Root cause.** `HTTPPacketConnection` is core-library code (`source/core/slang-http.*`), not slang-test-private. The retry mitigation I leaned on lives ONLY in slang-test's `_executeRPC`. I asserted the bound WITHOUT enumerating the class's consumers — a REACH over-claim (a plausible-sounding safety observation stated as fact). `search/code HTTPPacketConnection` → 7 hits: the def, `JSONRPCConnection`, and three tool consumers (`test-process-main.cpp`, `test-context.cpp`, `unit-test-process.cpp`). Spot-check: `test-process-main.cpp` has **0** retry/`recordTestServerLoss` references. So the mitigation does NOT cover the change's blast radius; the bound was false. This is the "a negative safety observation that could not have come out otherwise carries no bits" trap applied to a POSITIVE clear: I let a mitigation in one consumer clear a gap in shared code.

**How to catch it.** Before clearing a gap on a `source/core` or `source/compiler-core` change with the reason "the PR's own handling mitigates it", run `gh api search/code?q=<Class>+repo:...` and confirm EVERY consumer gets the mitigation. A mitigation that lives in one caller cannot bound the blast radius of a change to shared code. The tell is a past-/present-tense claim about coverage ("is bounded", "self-mitigated", "safe direction") that I did not derive from an enumeration — that sentence IS the trigger to open the consumer list. Conservative-lean: a core-library change with a real trigger and multiple non-retrying consumers, whose positive path is untested, is OPEN_GAP, not a clear.
